Food additives

Modified starches (E1404-E1452)

In brief

Modified starches are starches treated with chemicals so that they thicken, gel or stabilise foods such as desserts and mayonnaise. The gut digests them nearly as well as ordinary starch, and bacteria in the colon ferment the remainder. Regulators found no safety concern at current use, and in a French cohort a mixture of additives that included them was linked with slightly more type 2 diabetes.

What it is

Modified starches are starches altered chemically to thicken, gel, stabilise or emulsify foods such as desserts and mayonnaise 12.

The EU authorises twelve, E1404 to E1452, made by oxidation, phosphorylation, cross-linking, acetylation, hydroxypropylation or octenyl succination, alone or combined; E1404 to E1451 have identical INS numbers 1.

How often it is used

Modified starches are the third commonest additive in French food products, listed on 10,100 of 126,556 in 2019 3.

Individual starches are undercounted, since “modified starch” alone may be declared on labels 31. Of NutriNet-Santé adults, 91.48% consumed them, a mean 1596.45 mg/day or 24.33 mg/kg body weight/day, against 112.0 in EFSA’s French non-brand-loyal scenario 4.

In the body

Modified starches are not absorbed intact: digestive enzymes split them, and colonic bacteria ferment the rest 1.

EFSA’s fibre definition lists chemically modified starches under resistant starch 5. Fermentation yields acetate, propionate and butyrate, the main colonocyte fuel, formed mainly via the acetyl-CoA pathway 16.

What studies show

In a French cohort, people eating more of a mixture of modified starches, pectin, guar gum, carrageenan, polyphosphates, potassium sorbate, curcumin and xanthan gum developed type 2 diabetes slightly more often 7.

Over a mean 7.7 years, the hazard ratio was 1.08 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.15) per standard deviation; 465 of 36,214 in the highest third and 260 of 36,215 in the lowest developed it 7. Rats fed up to 31,000 mg/kg body weight/day showed no effects relevant to humans; adults tolerated one 25,000 mg dose of E1450 1.

Industry-funded results

In a 15-adult crossover trial sponsored by MGP Ingredients, maker of the cross-linked phosphorylated wheat starch tested, with an MGP co-author, the primary 0–2 h incremental glucose area was 106 ± 16 mmol·min/L, against 124 ± 16 with control cookies (mean ± SEM; P = 0.087) 8.

Unresolved questions

Causality cannot be established; adjusted for modified-starch intake, the mixture hazard ratio was 1.06 (1.00 to 1.12) 7. Infant faecal cultures fermented maize hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate with more gas, a faster pH fall and a less varied microbiota than locust bean gum; funding is undetermined 9. EFSA assessed six infant trials of E1450, all industry submissions not designed to test it 10.

Regulation

The EU and the United States permit modified starches; EFSA, and Japan’s Food Safety Commission for all but E1452, set no numerical acceptable daily intake 1112.

EFSA’s refined brand-loyal 95th-percentile exposure estimate was 3,053 mg/kg body weight/day, in toddlers 1. For E1450, EFSA found no indication of concern in infants below 16 weeks up to 2,725 mg/kg body weight/day 10. China lists ten, not E1451 or E1452, mostly for use as production requires 12.

  5. Regulator

    EFSA Panel on Dietetic Products, Nutrition, and Allergies (NDA). Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for carbohydrates and dietary fibre EFSA Journal, 2010.

    doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2010.1462

    Funding and finding
    Funding
    European Food Safety Authority, on request from the European Commission (Question EFSA-Q-2008-467).
    Finding
    Dietary fibre is defined as non-digestible carbohydrates plus lignin: non-starch polysaccharides (cellulose, hemicelluloses, pectins, hydrocolloids such as gums, mucilages and beta-glucans), resistant oligosaccharides, resistant starch (physically enclosed starch, some raw starch granules, retrograded amylose, modified starches) and associated lignin. In cereals cellulose occurs with hemicelluloses; lignified outer layers are the predominant fibre source in whole-grain products; oats and barley contain high concentrations of water-soluble, viscous beta-glucan. The soluble/insoluble distinction is method-dependent and does not always predict physiological effects. Salivary and pancreatic amylase degrade starch mainly to maltose and oligosaccharides, brush-border disaccharidases release glucose, and glucose is absorbed by the sodium-coupled SGLT1; monosaccharide absorption is regarded as rate-limiting. GI: incremental area under the blood glucose curve over 1.5-3 h after a 50 g carbohydrate portion, as a percentage of the response to the same amount of carbohydrate from glucose or white bread in the same subject; GL = glycaemic carbohydrate x GI/100. Amylose/amylopectin ratio and gelatinisation affect GI; intact cereal grains, parboiled rice and the protein network of pasta lower it. Fibre enters the colon substantially unmodified and is fermented to short-chain fatty acids; acetate and propionate are absorbed and provide energy; butyrate is a main source of energy for the colonic mucosa. Inhibitory effects on iron, zinc and calcium absorption have been attributed to complexing compounds, notably phytic acid in cereals. Wheat bran fibre gives 5 to 6 g faecal bulk per g. Energy factor 8 kJ (2 kcal) per g fibre. Adequate intake: 25 g fibre per day for normal laxation in adults; 2 g per MJ for children from 1 year.

    Vital M, Howe AC, Tiedje JM. Revealing the bacterial butyrate synthesis pathways by analyzing (meta)genomic data mBio, 2014.

    doi:10.1128/mBio.00889-14PubMed 24757212

    Funding and finding
    Funding
    Financial support was provided by the NIH Human Microbiome Project Demonstration Project (UH3 DK083993).
    Finding
    225 genomes carried a butyrate synthesis pathway. Four pathways (acetyl-CoA, glutarate, 4-aminobutyrate, lysine) converge on the conversion of crotonyl-CoA to butyryl-CoA by the butyryl-CoA dehydrogenase–electron-transferring flavoprotein complex, a step postulated to conserve energy through ferredoxin reduction. In the acetyl-CoA pathway, thiolase, β-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase and crotonase precede this step, and butyryl-CoA:acetate CoA-transferase or phosphate butyryltransferase with butyrate kinase forms butyrate. In stool metagenomes the acetyl-CoA pathway made up a mean 79.7% of butyrate pathways; for that pathway the terminal genes but and buk had median abundances of 77.2% and 21.8%. Butyrate is described as the main energy source for colonocytes.

    1,131 participants were diagnosed with type 2 diabetes. Nonnegative matrix factorisation identified five additive mixtures.     Finding
    JECFA had evaluated starch sodium octenyl succinate for use as an emulsifier in infant formula. Six clinical trials in infants below 16 weeks were submitted by interested business operators; none was planned to investigate the influence of E 1450 on body weight or its tolerability; five were rated tier 3 (high risk of bias) and one tier 2. Table A.1, mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids (单,双甘油脂肪酸酯, CNS 10.006, INS 471), functions emulsifier and coating agent (乳化剂、被膜剂): according to production needs in various foods, except the 59 categories numbered 1 to 4, 6 to 11, 13 to 14, 16 to 30, 32 to 53 and 59 to 68 in Table A.2; cream (5), surface-treated fresh fruit (12) and vegetables (15), raw wet noodle products (31) and infant formulae and complementary foods for infants (54 to 58) are not among the exceptions; maximum 20.0 g/kg in butter and concentrated butter (02.02.01.01), 30.0 g/kg in raw dry noodle products (06.03.02.02), 6.0 g/kg in brown sugar, raw sugar, other sugars and syrups (11.01.02) and 5.0 g/kg in spices (12.09). Table A.1, phosphoric acid and phosphates (磷酸及磷酸盐), a group entry that includes disodium dihydrogen pyrophosphate (焦磷酸二氢二钠), tetrasodium pyrophosphate (焦磷酸钠), tetrapotassium pyrophosphate (焦磷酸四钾), trisodium monohydrogen diphosphate (焦磷酸一氢三钠) and calcium acid pyrophosphate (酸式焦磷酸钙); its INS numbers include 450(i), 450(ii), 450(iii), 450(v) and 450(vii); functions water-retention agent, raising agent, acidity regulator, stabiliser, coagulant and anticaking agent (水分保持剂、膨松剂、酸度调节剂、稳定剂、凝固剂、抗结剂).
