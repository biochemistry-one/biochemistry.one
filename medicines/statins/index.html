Medicines

Statins

In brief

Statins can cause muscle breakdown that injures the kidneys, an autoimmune muscle disease that may persist after stopping, rare liver failure, bleeding into the brain and new diabetes. The enzyme they block also supplies coenzyme Q10, haem A, dolichol and lipid groups attached to proteins, in muscle and brain as well as liver. In people without cardiovascular disease, one death is avoided for every 286 treated for up to 6 years; in people over 70, a trial found fewer cardiovascular events without longer survival free of dementia and disability.

Statins are prescribed to lower LDL cholesterol and the risk of heart attack and stroke 1.

Unresolved questions

The Cholesterol Treatment Trialists’ Collaboration received individual data only for its own meta-analyses and sought only myopathy that had progressed to rhabdomyolysis 132. A few trials used an active run-in that may exclude people with early adverse effects; BMJ editors stated that the trials were not designed specifically to measure harms 1415.

Other pathways it affects

The enzyme that statins block is found in every tissue and supplies more than cholesterol 16.

Its downstream intermediates supply:

  • coenzyme Q10, which transports electrons in mitochondria and requires COQ2, expressed most in skeletal muscle, adrenal glands and heart 1716;
  • haem A of cytochrome c oxidase, through COX10 and COX15, the latter mainly in oxidative tissues such as muscle, heart and brain 16;
  • dolichol phosphate, to which sugars are attached for protein glycosylation 16;
  • farnesyl and geranylgeranyl groups attached to proteins 17;
  • a tRNA modification by TRIT1, which is required for selenoprotein expression 16.

In confirmed statin myalgia, serum coenzyme Q10 fell from 1.3 to 0.8 µg/mL in 8 weeks on simvastatin 20 mg; supplements of 400 or 600 mg/day did not reduce myalgia in independent trials 1819. Biallelic HMGCR variants cause limb-girdle muscular dystrophy 28; in the first family, the authors describe the muscle imaging as highly reminiscent of end-stage statin myopathy 1620. LDL-lowering HMGCR alleles were associated with type 2 diabetes (odds ratio 1.029 per allele) 21.

The central nervous system, 2.1% of body weight, holds 23% of the body’s sterol; the cholesterol for its growth apparently comes exclusively from local synthesis 22. In rare reports, memory loss reversed on stopping (median 3 weeks) 8; in 9971 people aged 70 or older, atorvastatin 40 mg did not change the composite of death, dementia or persistent physical disability (HR 0.94, 0.84 to 1.05) 23.

Industry-funded results

In 25 patients, 28 days of atorvastatin 80 mg lowered leukocyte Rho kinase activity by about 49% against placebo, uncorrelated with LDL change, which the authors attribute to fewer isoprenoids 24.

Unresolved questions

In 11 trials, mostly by industry-affiliated or industry-funded teams, statins lowered testosterone by 0.66 nmol/L in 501 men and 0.40 nmol/L in 368 women with polycystic ovary syndrome 25.

What it is for and how it works

People who inherit lower LDL cholesterol develop less coronary heart disease 26.

Statins competitively inhibit HMG-CoA reductase 27. In cells and animals, lower cholesterol in the endoplasmic reticulum releases SREBP-2, which activates the LDL receptor gene, so liver cells remove more LDL 2829.

In the body

Atorvastatin, rosuvastatin and simvastatin are taken up into the liver by a transporter protein, OATP1B1 27130.

CYP3A4 metabolises atorvastatin and simvastatin, but not rosuvastatin to a clinically significant extent 27130. Simvastatin is contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, ciclosporin, danazol and gemfibrozil 5. The MHRA advises against systemic fusidic acid with statins after suspected fatal rhabdomyolysis 31.

Genetic variation

Ancestry and inherited variants alter blood statin levels and muscle risk 323.

In pharmacokinetic studies, Asian subjects had about twice the median rosuvastatin exposure of White controls 32. On simvastatin 40 mg over a median 3.9 years, myopathy occurred in about 0.24% of 5468 Chinese and 0.05% of 7367 non-Chinese patients 5.

Without the medicine

In 7447 Spanish adults at high risk, a Mediterranean diet with extra virgin olive oil or nuts lowered heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death from 4.4% to 3.8% and 3.4% over a median 4.8 years, without a difference in all-cause mortality 36. The first report was retracted for randomisation irregularities; this reanalysis gave similar results 36.

How the recommendations were set

Of 288 members of 14 US and Canadian lipid and diabetes guideline panels from 2000 to 2010, 52% had financial conflicts of interest 37.

Consensus statements

The 2004 US update made LDL cholesterol below 70 mg/dL an option for very high risk; eight of its nine authors disclosed ties to manufacturers of lipid-lowering drugs 38.

Stopping

In an open-label trial in people aged 75 or older taking a statin to prevent a first heart attack or stroke, 35 of 484 (7.2%) died within 3 years after stopping and 48 of 604 (7.9%) with continuation 39.

In an unblinded trial in people with a life expectancy of 1 month to 1 year, 60-day mortality was 45 of 189 (23.8%) after stopping and 39 of 192 (20.3%) with continuation, so non-inferiority was not shown 40.

A prescription is changed only together with the prescriber.

    Finding
    Simvastatin is a lactone prodrug hydrolysed after administration to its active beta-hydroxyacid form, simvastatin acid; simvastatin acid and its metabolites inhibit HMG-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme that converts HMG-CoA to mevalonate. Inhibition of HMG-CoA reductase by simvastatin acid accelerates the expression of LDL receptors, followed by uptake of LDL cholesterol from blood into the liver and a fall in plasma LDL cholesterol. Because simvastatin undergoes extensive first-pass extraction in the liver, its availability to the general circulation is below 5%. Simvastatin is metabolised by CYP3A4, and simvastatin acid is a substrate of the transport protein OATP1B1. Contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and with ciclosporin, danazol or gemfibrozil. In clinical studies of 24,747 simvastatin-treated patients (median follow-up 4 years), myopathy (muscle symptoms with creatine kinase above 10 times the upper limit of normal) occurred in about 0.03%, 0.08% and 0.61% on 20, 40 and 80 mg daily; in a study of 12,064 patients with previous myocardial infarction (mean 6.7 years), in about 0.02% on 20 mg and 0.9% on 80 mg.     Finding
    There is sufficient evidence to support an association between statin use and new-onset diabetes. In a 2010 trial meta-analysis (Sattar et al.) the odds ratio was 1.09 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.17), and treatment of 255 (150 to 852) patients for 4 years resulted in one extra case.
