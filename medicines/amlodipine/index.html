Amlodipine

In brief

Amlodipine can lower blood pressure too far, worsen chest pain or bring on a heart attack when the dose is started or raised, and in overdose cause shock and fluid in the lungs; in trials it led to more heart failure than a diuretic. Its target channel is also found in the heart, brain, gut, bladder and pancreas, and the drug commonly makes the ankles swell. Lowering blood pressure with drugs prevented 14 to 38 heart attacks, strokes and other cardiovascular events per 1000 people treated for 5 years, more at higher risk.

Industry-funded results

In Pfizer-funded trials in severe heart failure, pulmonary oedema was more frequent on amlodipine; in non-ischaemic disease, deaths were 278 against 262 on placebo, of 827 each, over a median 33 months (hazard ratio 1.09) 892. In AASK (1094 African Americans with hypertensive kidney disease; part-funded by Pfizer, AstraZeneca and King), the amlodipine arm was stopped for safety; ramipril cut a large GFR fall, kidney failure or death by 38% (87 of 436 against 56 of 217) 1011.

Unresolved questions

The EMA advises caution in heart failure, as this class may increase events and mortality; the US label reports safe use in well-compensated heart failure 42. Independent reviewers state that harms were under-measured and possibly unreported, in patients less frail than in practice 3.

Unresolved questions

Both regulators record possible extrapyramidal disorder; whether therapeutic doses block Cav1.3, annotated in the basal ganglia, is not addressed in these sources 2412. Across 260,447 trial participants the class was associated with cancer against all comparators (hazard ratio 1.06, 1.01 to 1.11), not against placebo 16.

What it is for and how it works

Calcium entering artery-wall muscle makes it contract; amlodipine blocks the channel that admits it 1.

The channel is Cav1.2 (CACNA1C) 17. Ionised at physiological pH (pKa 8.6), amlodipine binds gradually, hence the slow onset 1.

In the body

The liver breaks amlodipine down slowly, so one tablet a day suffices 1.

The half-life is 30 to 50 hours, or 56 in hepatic impairment; metabolism, mainly by CYP3A4 and CYP3A5, inactivates about 90% of a dose 1218. Erythromycin and clarithromycin were associated with admission for hypotension or shock in older users of this class (odds ratios 5.8 and 3.7), unlike azithromycin, which does not inhibit CYP3A4 19. Amlodipine weakly inhibits CYP3A and raised simvastatin exposure 77%, so simvastatin is limited to 20 mg daily 24. From animal data, EU information advises against calcium channel blockers with dantrolene in malignant hyperthermia or susceptibility to it 4.

Genetic variation

Rare changes in the channel genes cause heart-rhythm and hormone disorders; a liver-enzyme variant raises drug levels 1218.

CACNA1C variants cause Timothy syndrome, with intermittent hypoglycaemia, and long QT syndrome 8; CACNA1D variants cause sinoatrial node dysfunction with deafness, and activating ones primary aldosteronism with seizures 12. CYP3A4*22 carriers had dose-adjusted levels 1.94 nmol/l per mg higher 18.

Against no treatment, evidence for this class is one trial of a related drug, judged of lower quality 20.

Industry-funded results

In ACCOMPLISH, analysed by Novartis, events were 9.6% with benazepril plus amlodipine against 11.8% with hydrochlorothiazide over 36 months (hazard ratio 0.80) 25.

Without the medicine

A potassium-containing salt substitute reduced strokes and deaths in a randomised trial 26.

In 20,995 rural Chinese adults with prior stroke, or aged 60 or older with poorly controlled pressure, it cut stroke from 33.65 to 29.14 and death from 44.61 to 39.28 per 1000 person-years over a mean 4.74 years; households using potassium-sparing drugs or with serious kidney disease were excluded 26.

Unresolved questions

Exercise trials measured only resting pressure, and no weight-loss trial had mortality as a predefined outcome 2728.

How the recommendations were set

In 2003 a US panel created the category of pre-hypertension; 9 of its 11 members disclosed industry ties 29.

Eight had ties to Novartis, maker of amlodipine with benazepril; none of 16 disease-definition publications rigorously assessed harms of the changes 29. The 2017 ACC/AHA definition of 130/80 mm Hg raised estimated US adult prevalence from 31.9% to 45.6% 30.

Consensus statements

In 2014 JNC 8 raised the threshold and goal at 60 or older to 150/90 mm Hg, without unanimity 31. The 2024 ESC guideline, funded by the ESC alone, advises drugs from 130/80 mm Hg at a 10-year risk of 10% or more, after 3 months of lifestyle measures 32.

Stopping

Removing one blood-pressure drug at 80 or older kept most people’s pressure controlled for 12 weeks 33.

In OPTiMISE (569 people), control was 86.4% against 87.7%, and serious adverse events 4.3% against 2.4% (RR 1.72, 0.7 to 4.3) 33. Withdrawing a calcium channel blocker raised systolic pressure by 5 mm Hg (0 to 10) in an exploratory analysis 34.

Unresolved questions

Six withdrawal trials in 1073 older people allowed no firm conclusions: mortality OR 2.08 (0.79 to 5.46), and no trial reported falls 35.

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.

Mechanism: a dihydropyridine calcium antagonist that inhibits the transmembrane influx of calcium ions into vascular smooth muscle and cardiac muscle, selectively and with a greater effect on vascular smooth muscle than on cardiac muscle; within the physiological pH range it is ionised (pKa 8.6) and its kinetic interaction with the calcium channel receptor is characterised by a gradual rate of association and dissociation, resulting in a gradual onset of effect; a peripheral arterial vasodilator that reduces peripheral vascular resistance; serum calcium concentration is not affected; sinoatrial nodal function and atrioventricular conduction are not changed.     McEvoy JW, McCarthy CP, Bruno RM, Brouwers S, Canavan MD, Ceconi C, et al.; ESC Scientific Document Group. 2024 ESC Guidelines for the management of elevated blood pressure and hypertension European Heart Journal, 2024.

    doi:10.1093/eurheartj/ehae178PubMed 39210715

    Funding
    Funding for the development of ESC Guidelines is derived entirely from the ESC with no involvement of the healthcare industry.
    Finding
    Office blood pressure categories: non-elevated below 120/70 mm Hg; elevated 120 to 139 systolic or 70 to 89 diastolic; hypertension 140 or above systolic or 90 or above diastolic, unchanged as a definition.
