Medicines

ACE inhibitors and angiotensin receptor blockers

In brief

These medicines can cause swelling of the lips, tongue and throat that can block breathing, a dangerous rise in blood potassium, sudden kidney failure, and injury or death of an unborn child exposed in later pregnancy. The enzyme that ACE inhibitors block also breaks down bradykinin and other signalling molecules in many tissues, which is presumed to cause a persistent dry cough, and blocking the hormone chain raises renin and alters potassium handling and the risk of gout. In trials in high blood pressure, ACE inhibitors lowered deaths over about five years from 13.6% to 11.3%; in mild high blood pressure without heart disease, the effect on deaths was uncertain.

Serious harms

ACE inhibitors such as lisinopril and angiotensin receptor blockers such as losartan are prescribed for high blood pressure, heart failure, after myocardial infarction and for diabetic kidney disease 12.

Industry-funded results

In ALLHAT, with Pfizer support, angio-oedema affected 38 of 9054 on lisinopril against 8 of 15,255 on chlorthalidone over a mean 4.9 years, with one death 15.

Unresolved questions

That bradykinin mediates angio-oedema rests on plasma levels in one patient; icatibant, a bradykinin-receptor blocker, outperformed corticosteroid plus antihistamine in 27 patients but not placebo in 121 161718. First-trimester malformation risk rose in one cohort but not against hypertensive mothers in two larger ones; the EMA cannot exclude a small risk for either class 19202122.

Other pathways it affects

ACE, found in most tissues, breaks down several signalling peptides 23.

  • Peptides (reference database): bradykinin, substance P, neurotensin, enkephalins, cholecystokinin and amyloid-beta; a testis isoform is required for male fertility 23.
  • Airways (people): cough, presumed from bradykinin, affected almost 12% over a year on ramipril, resolving on stopping; in head-to-head trials it caused 22 of 51 stated withdrawals, against 2 of 49 on receptor blockers 524.
  • Kidney (people): angiotensin II constricts the efferent arteriole; creatinine rose 30% or more in 1.7% of 122,363 starters, associated with death (rate ratio 1.84) 25.
  • Renin (labels): renin activity doubles to triples on losartan 2.
  • Urate (people): gout relative risk 0.81 on losartan, 1.24 on ACE inhibitors 26.
  • Glucose (trials, labels): new type 2 diabetes relative risk 0.84 against placebo for each class; irbesartan may induce hypoglycaemia 2722.

Unresolved questions

The enalapril label lists cough in 1.3%; 125 trials gave a pooled 11.48% 428.

What it is for and how it works

These medicines lower blood pressure by blocking a hormone chain that constricts arteries and retains salt 1213.

ACE inhibitors lower angiotensin II formation; losartan binds the AT1 receptor with about 1000-fold greater affinity than AT2 12. ACE2, not inhibited by ACE inhibitors, converts angiotensin II to angiotensin-(1-7) 23.

In the body

Combining two drugs that block this chain adds harm without lowering deaths 29.

Across 33 trials (68,405 patients, mean 52 weeks), mortality was unchanged (relative risk 0.97) and heart-failure admissions fell by 18%, while hyperkalaemia rose from 4.9% to 9.6% and renal failure by 41% 29.

Genetic variation

Inherited defects in any of four components of this chain disrupt kidney development before birth 23.

Variants in REN, AGT, ACE or AGTR1 cause renal tubular dysgenesis 23.

Benefit against harm

In three placebo-controlled trials in hypertension (6002 patients), ACE inhibitors lowered deaths over 4.9 years from 13.6% to 11.3% (relative risk 0.83), one per 43 treated 30.

No such trial of a first-line receptor blocker was found 30.

Unresolved questions

Trandolapril did not reduce events in 8290 lower-risk coronary patients over 4.8 years (21.9% against 22.5%) 32. Withdrawals for adverse effects could not be calculated for first-line ACE inhibitors 30.

Without the medicine

In a randomised trial, replacing part of the salt with potassium salt lowered stroke and death 33.

Among 20,995 Chinese villagers, 72.6% with previous stroke, stroke fell from 33.65 to 29.14 and death from 44.61 to 39.28 per 1000 person-years over 4.74 years 33. The trial excluded households using potassium-sparing diuretics or supplements and did not measure serum potassium, though 22.8% took these drugs 33.

How the recommendations were set

Lowering the US threshold from 140/90 to 130/80 mm Hg in 2017 raised the estimated share of adults with high blood pressure from 31.9% to 45.6% 34.

The guideline’s own writing committee produced the estimate 34.

Consensus statements

NICE offers a calcium channel blocker first from age 55 and for people of Black African or African-Caribbean origin without type 2 diabetes, adding these drugs at step 2 or 3 35.

Unresolved questions

At least 80% of authors of four ESC guidelines, heart failure among them, had financial conflicts 36.

Stopping

Stopping these drugs 48 hours before major surgery did not change death or major complications 37.

In 2222 patients, rates were 22% in each group at 28 days; intraoperative hypotension was 54% when continued against 41% 37. With eGFR below 30 ml/min/1.73 m², stopping did not preserve kidney function over 3 years (411 patients) 38. In recovered dilated cardiomyopathy, phased withdrawal of heart-failure drugs led to relapse in 11 of 25 within 6 months, against none of 26 39.

Unresolved questions

Labels state that abrupt withdrawal has not been associated with a rapid rise in pressure; withdrawal trials did not report falls 140.

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.

    Finding
    Discontinuation against continuation: all-cause mortality OR 2.08 (95% CI 0.79 to 5.46; 4 studies, 630 participants; low certainty), myocardial infarction 1.86 (0.19 to 17.98; very low certainty), hospitalisation 0.83 (0.33 to 2.10; 1 study, 385), stroke 1.44 (0.25 to 8.35; 3 studies, 524); systolic pressure 9.75 mm Hg higher (7.33 to 12.18) and diastolic 3.5 mm Hg higher. Adverse drug reactions could not be meta-analysed, withdrawal reactions were not assessed and no study reported falls. The authors conclude that firm conclusions cannot be drawn and call for trials in frail, older and polymedicated people measuring adverse drug events, falls and quality of life.
