Method

How evidence is graded

In brief

Each study cited on this site is assigned a class according to who paid for it and what part the payer played. Findings of independent research, regulators and reference works form the main text. Results of industry-funded studies, expert statements and open questions appear in separate labelled blocks.

Who paid for the study

  • IndependentPublic or charitable funding, no industry role
  • Industry-fundedAny industry money or role in design, analysis or publication
  • RegulatoryAssessments of FDA, EMA, EFSA and similar bodies
  • ConsensusGuidelines and expert panels
  • Reference workTextbooks and curated databases
  • Funding undeterminedNo funding statement could be read

Where a statement sits

  • Main text: independent findings and mechanisms
  • Industry-funded results
  • Consensus statements
  • Unresolved questions
