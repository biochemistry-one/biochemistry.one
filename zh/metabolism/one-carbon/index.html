叶酸、维生素B12与一碳单位代谢

要点

细胞利用叶酸和维生素B12，把单个碳原子转入胸腺嘧啶和S-腺苷甲硫氨酸。胸腺嘧啶是DNA的四个“字母”之一，S-腺苷甲硫氨酸则为DNA、蛋白质和脂质提供甲基。缺乏其中任何一种维生素时，骨髓无法正常复制DNA，血液中的红细胞数量过少，且体积大于正常。

叶酸的形式

人体必须从食物中获取叶酸1。细胞利用叶酸结合单个碳原子，用于合成DNA碱基和甲硫氨酸（一种氨基酸）1。

合成叶酸是添加到食品和补充剂中的形式，须经二氢叶酸还原酶（DHFR）还原为四氢叶酸（THF）后才具有活性21。

胸苷酸与嘌呤

正在分裂的细胞利用单个碳原子合成胸腺嘧啶（一种DNA碱基）和嘌呤碱基1。

胸苷酸合酶将5,10-亚甲基-THF上的碳原子转移至dUMP，生成dTMP和二氢叶酸（DHF）1。

甲氨蝶呤抑制DHFR，使叶酸以DHF形式滞留1。

MTHFR与677C>T变异

MTHFR是一种酶，可将叶酸库的一部分转化为甲基叶酸，后者为合成甲硫氨酸提供甲基3。编码该酶的基因有一种常见变异，即677C>T，可使该酶的稳定性降低3。

TT纯合子的细胞提取物中，该酶活性约为CC型提取物的30%3。欧洲裔人群和日本人中约10%～15%为TT纯合子3。该变异是轻度高同型半胱氨酸血症最常见的遗传原因3。

甲硫氨酸合酶与维生素B12

甲硫氨酸合酶的活性依赖维生素B124。该酶将甲基叶酸上的甲基转移给同型半胱氨酸，使甲硫氨酸再生，同时释放出叶酸42。

由于没有其他反应消耗甲基叶酸，而MTHFR催化的反应又不能逆向进行，维生素B12缺乏时甲基叶酸便会蓄积2。这种“甲基叶酸陷阱”造成功能性叶酸缺乏25。

S-腺苷甲硫氨酸与同型半胱氨酸

甲硫氨酸转化为S-腺苷甲硫氨酸，后者是主要的甲基供体6。每次甲基转移最终都会产生同型半胱氨酸；同型半胱氨酸或重新转化为甲硫氨酸，或分解为半胱氨酸6。

15项随机试验共纳入71,422名参与者，随访1～7.3年；结果显示，用维生素B6、B9（叶酸）或B12降低同型半胱氨酸未能预防心肌梗死7。治疗组和安慰剂组的心肌梗死发生率分别为7.1%和6.0%（相对危险度1.02，95%置信区间0.95～1.10）7。两组卒中发生率分别为4.3%和5.1%（0.90，0.82～0.99），未经校正的绝对差值为0.8个百分点，需治疗人数约为1257。

巨幼细胞贫血

缺乏叶酸或维生素B12时，骨髓无法正常合成DNA，血液中的红细胞数量过少，且体积大于正常12。

5,10-亚甲基-THF不足时，胸苷酸合成减慢，DNA聚合酶因而将尿嘧啶掺入DNA5。叶酸缺乏的成人（n = 8）血液DNA中的尿嘧啶数量为对照者（n = 14）的8倍5。维生素B12缺乏还可因脱髓鞘导致脊髓神经病，叶酸缺乏时则几乎见不到这种病变2。

企业资助研究的结果

糖尿病预防计划（Diabetes Prevention Program）的部分经费来自百时美施贵宝（Bristol-Myers Squibb）和帕克-戴维斯（Parke-Davis），该研究将成人分配至二甲双胍组（850 mg，每日2次）或安慰剂组8。平均5年后，二甲双胍组859人中有37人（4.3%）、安慰剂组856人中有20人（2.3%）血清维生素B12不高于203 pg/ml8。绝对差值为2.0个百分点，需伤害人数约为518。

共识声明

欧洲食品安全局将成人钴胺素的适宜摄入量定为每天4 µg9。该机构保留了成人每天1000 µg合成叶酸的可耐受最高摄入量，理由是合成叶酸可纠正维生素B12缺乏所致的贫血，神经损害却仍在进展2。

尚未解决的问题

同型半胱氨酸中度升高是否导致血管疾病，尚存争议1011。在19个未发表的数据集中，与CC基因型相比，MTHFR 677TT基因型的冠心病比值比为1.02（95%置信区间0.98～1.07）10。在86项已发表的研究中，这一比值比为1.15（1.09～1.21）10。作者将两者的差异归因于发表偏倚或方法学问题10。在237个遗传学数据集中，TT与CC基因型之间的同型半胱氨酸差值在亚洲为3.12 µmol/L（2.23～4.01），在实行叶酸强化的地区为0.13 µmol/L（−0.85～1.11）11。两类地区的卒中比值比分别为1.68（1.44～1.97）和1.03（0.84～1.25）11。截至该分析时，尚无试验专门在低叶酸地区检验降低同型半胱氨酸的效果11。

    Ducker GS, Rabinowitz JD. One-Carbon Metabolism in Health and Disease Cell Metabolism, 2017.

    doi:10.1016/j.cmet.2016.08.009PubMed 27641100

    资助与结果
    资助
    G.S.D. is supported by a postdoctoral fellowship from the American Cancer Society (PF-15-190-01-TBE). J.D.R. is supported by NIH grants R01CA163591 and P30DK019525 and Stand Up to Cancer.
    结果
    Review. Animals require dietary folate. Tetrahydrofolate (THF) binds one-carbon units at N5 and N10 at three oxidation states: 5-methyl-THF, the most reduced, 5,10-methylene-THF and 10-formyl-THF, the most oxidised; folic acid is reduced to dihydrofolate (DHF) and then THF. SHMT1 (cytosol) and SHMT2 (mitochondria) interconvert serine and glycine with THF and 5,10-methylene-THF, reversibly; in rapidly proliferating cultured cells the main direct source of cytosolic one-carbon units is formate released from mitochondria, reassimilated by the trifunctional MTHFD1. TYMS methylates dUMP to dTMP with 5,10-methylene-THF and is the only folate enzyme that alters THF itself, taking two electrons from it and producing DHF; DHFR regenerates THF with NADPH and is the target of trimethoprim and methotrexate, which trap folate as DHF. Purine synthesis incorporates two 10-formyl-THF units; in proliferating cells each new DNA or RNA base needs on average one 10-formyl-THF, and 5,10-methylene-THF is needed for one in four DNA bases. 5-Fluorouracil is a suicide inhibitor of TYMS; methotrexate most potently blocks DHFR, whereas pemetrexed more potently inhibits TYMS and AICAR transformylase; 5-formyl-THF (folinic acid, leucovorin) is given as rescue. 5-Methyl-THF, made by cytosolic NADPH-dependent MTHFR, is consumed only by cobalamin-dependent methionine synthase; BHMT is limited to liver and kidney. SAM inhibits MTHFR and activates CBS; 5-methyl-THF inhibits GNMT. Transsulfuration is the only route that degrades homocysteine. Folate and cobalamin supplementation lower homocysteine without reducing cardiovascular events in the general population. Dietary folate deficiency in adults manifests as macrocytic anaemia.

  2. 监管机构

    EFSA Panel on Nutrition, Novel Foods and Food Allergens (NDA Panel), Turck D, Bohn T, Castenmiller J, de Henauw S, Hirsch-Ernst KI, et al. Scientific opinion on the tolerable upper intake level for folate EFSA Journal, 2023.

    doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2023.8353PubMed 37965303

    资助与结果
    资助
    European Food Safety Authority, on request from the European Commission (question EFSA-Q-2021-00366). Preparatory systematic reviews were conducted by a contractor, Karolinska Institutet in collaboration with the University of Oslo, under an EFSA procurement procedure.
    结果
    Folates consist of a pterin moiety linked to p-aminobenzoic acid and one or more glutamates, substituted at N5 or N10 by one-carbon units; natural folates are reduced polyglutamates usually with five to seven glutamates. A jejunal hydrolase (EC 3.4.17.21) converts polyglutamates to monoglutamates, which PCFT (SLC46A1), the most important intestinal transporter, and RFC (SLC19A1) move into enterocytes; 5-methyl-THF is the main circulating form; RFC, the main transporter from blood to tissues, has about 100 times lower affinity for folic acid than for reduced folates. Folylpolyglutamate synthase retains folates as THF polyglutamates; 5-methyl-THF monoglutamate and folic acid are poor substrates, and 5-methyl-THF is lost from cells unless methionine synthase converts it to THF. Folic acid, a fully oxidised monoglutamate, is reduced by DHFR via dihydrofolate to THF. The MTHFR reaction is essentially irreversible under physiological conditions and SAM is its allosteric inhibitor; in cobalamin deficiency 5-methyl-THF is metabolically trapped, SAM falls, MTHFR becomes fully active, and a functional folate deficiency develops with raised plasma 5-methyl-THF and homocysteine, depleted erythrocyte folate and fewer methyl groups for transmethylation. The megaloblastic anaemia of cobalamin deficiency is identical to that of folate deficiency; neurological manifestations (myeloneuropathy from demyelination) can occur with or without anaemia, are almost never seen in folate deficiency, and have an unknown mechanism, with congenital defects of MTHFR and methionine synthase pointing to methionine synthase rather than methylmalonyl-CoA mutase. Folic acid can initiate DNA synthesis in cobalamin deficiency through DHFR but cannot correct the methylation defect. MTHFR 677TT is associated with lower serum folate and higher homocysteine; in a genome-wide study of 2232 Irish adults aged 18 to 28 years MTHFR 677C>T was the only significant modifier of red cell and serum folate. ULs for folic acid retained: 1000 ug/day for adults including pregnant and lactating women, with progression of neurological symptoms in cobalamin-deficient patients as critical effect.

  3. 独立研究

    Yamada K, Chen Z, Rozen R, Matthews RG. Effects of common polymorphisms on the properties of recombinant human methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 2001.

    doi:10.1073/pnas.261469998PubMed 11742092

    资助与结果
    资助
    These studies were supported in part by National Institutes of Health Grant GM24908 (to R.G.M.) and by funds from the Medical Research Council of Canada (to R.R.).
    结果
    Human MTHFR is a dimer of about 70 kDa subunits, each with non-covalently bound FAD, that reduces 5,10-methylene-THF to 5-methyl-THF with NADPH; SAM binds the C-terminal regulatory domain as an allosteric inhibitor, and SAH competes with SAM there without inhibiting. Purified recombinant Ala222Val enzyme (677C>T) had the same Km for NADPH and 5,10-methylene-THF, the same Vmax and the same SAM inhibition as wild type, but released FAD about 3-fold faster after dilution to 50 nM subunits at 37 degrees C; 5-methyl-THF and SAM slowed FAD loss. Background cited: cell extracts from TT homozygotes have about 30% of CC activity; about 10-15% of Caucasians of European origin and of Japanese are TT, more in Mediterranean countries and fewer among African Americans; 677C>T is the most frequent genetic cause of mild hyperhomocysteinaemia, seen in TT homozygotes with plasma folate below the median.

  4. 独立研究

    Gherasim C, Lofgren M, Banerjee R. Navigating the B12 Road: Assimilation, Delivery, and Disorders of Cobalamin Journal of Biological Chemistry, 2013.

    doi:10.1074/jbc.R113.458810PubMed 23539619

    资助与结果
    资助
    This work was supported, in whole or in part, by National Institutes of Health Grant DK45776.
    结果
    Review. Mammals have two B12-dependent enzymes: methionine synthase, which uses methylcobalamin, and methylmalonyl-CoA mutase, which uses adenosylcobalamin to convert methylmalonyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA. Methionine synthase works in two half-reactions: methylcobalamin methylates homocysteine to methionine, leaving cob(I)alamin, which removes the methyl group from 5-methyl-THF to give THF and methylcobalamin; occasional oxidation to cob(II)alamin is repaired by reductive methylation needing NADPH, methionine synthase reductase and S-adenosylmethionine. Food cobalamin binds haptocorrin; in the duodenum pancreatic proteases release it, intrinsic factor binds it, and intestinal cells take it up by cubilin/AMN receptor-mediated endocytosis. About 20% of circulating cobalamin is bound to transcobalamin, which delivers it to cells through the transcobalamin receptor. Mutations in the cblG (methionine synthase) and cblE (reductase) loci cause isolated hyperhomocysteinaemia without methylmalonic aciduria.

  5. 独立研究

    Blount BC, Mack MM, Wehr CM, MacGregor JT, Hiatt RA, Wang G, et al. Folate deficiency causes uracil misincorporation into human DNA and chromosome breakage: implications for cancer and neuronal damage Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America, 1997.

    doi:10.1073/pnas.94.7.3290PubMed 9096386

    资助与结果
    资助
    This work was supported by National Cancer Institute Outstanding Investigator Grant CA39910, by National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences Center Grant ESO1896 (B.N.A.), and by the intramural programs of the National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences and National Health and Environmental Effects Research Laboratory (R.B.E.). B.C.B. thanks the Biomedical Mass Spectrometry Unit at the University of New South Wales for financial support during the writing of a portion of the article.
    结果
    Folate-deficient adults (erythrocyte folate below 140 ng/ml, n = 8) had a geometric mean of 3,960,000 uracils per diploid cell in blood DNA, 8-fold more than controls (498,000, n = 14; P = 0.003); after 5 mg folic acid daily, measured at 6 to 8 weeks, the value fell to 186,000 (n = 5; P = 0.0003). Mechanism proposed: low 5,10-methylene-THF decreases dTMP synthesis, raises the dUMP/dTMP ratio and leads to DNA polymerase-mediated dUTP misincorporation; excision by uracil-DNA glycosylase and apyrimidinic endonuclease creates transient nicks, and two opposing nicks can form a double-strand break. Vitamin B12 deficiency traps folate as 5-methyl-THF, causing functional folate deficiency, homocysteine accumulation and uracil misincorporation.

  6. 独立研究

    Lu SC, Mato JM. S-adenosylmethionine in liver health, injury, and cancer Physiological Reviews, 2012.

    doi:10.1152/physrev.00047.2011PubMed 23073625

    资助与结果
    资助
    This work was supported by National Institutes of Health Grants R01DK51719, R01DK092407 and P30DK48522 (to S. C. Lu), R01AT1576 and R01AT004896 (to S. C. Lu and J. M. Mato) as well as Plan Nacional of I+D SAF 2011-29851 and Departamento de Educación Gobierno Vasco 2011 (to J. M. Mato).
    结果
    Review. Methionine adenosyltransferase synthesises S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) from methionine and ATP; tripolyphosphate formed in the reaction is hydrolysed to orthophosphate and pyrophosphate before product release. MAT1A is largely expressed in normal liver and MAT2A in all extrahepatic tissues. Over 200 human proteins are known or putative SAM-dependent methyltransferases; GNMT is the most abundant in liver (1% of cytosolic protein); the liver carries out up to 85% of all methylation reactions. S-adenosylhomocysteine (SAH) inhibits methyltransferases with a Ki in the micromolar range of their Km for SAM. AHCY cleaves SAH reversibly into adenosine and homocysteine; the thermodynamics favour SAH synthesis, and hydrolysis proceeds only if adenosine and homocysteine are rapidly removed. Homocysteine is remethylated by methionine synthase, expressed in all tissues, or by BHMT, limited to liver and kidney, with betaine; transsulfuration converts it to cysteine through CBS and cystathionase, both requiring vitamin B6. SAM inhibits MTHFR and activates CBS.

  7. 独立研究

    Martí-Carvajal AJ, Solà I, Lathyris D, Dayer M. Homocysteine-lowering interventions for preventing cardiovascular events Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2017.

    doi:10.1002/14651858.CD006612.pub5PubMed 28816346

    资助与结果
    参与者
    71,422
    随访
    1 to 7.3 years
    资助
    Internal sources: no sources of support provided. External sources: Iberoamerican Cochrane Centre, Spain (academic); Cochrane Heart Group, UK (academic).
    结果
    15 RCTs, 71,422 participants, follow-up 1 to 7.3 years. Against placebo, homocysteine-lowering interventions showed no difference in myocardial infarction (7.1% against 6.0%; RR 1.02, 95% CI 0.95-1.10; 12 trials, 46,699 participants), death from any cause (11.7% against 12.3%; RR 1.01, 0.96-1.06; 11 trials, 44,817) or serious adverse events (8.3% against 8.5%; RR 1.07, 1.00-1.14), and were associated with reduced stroke (4.3% against 5.1%; RR 0.90, 0.82-0.99; 10 trials, 44,224). Background: normal total homocysteine is close to 10 umol/L; fasting 12-30 umol/L is termed mild or moderate, 31-100 intermediate and above 100 severe hyperhomocysteinaemia.

    Clarke R, Bennett DA, Parish S, Verhoef P, Dötsch-Klerk M, Lathrop M, et al. Homocysteine and coronary heart disease: meta-analysis of MTHFR case-control studies, avoiding publication bias PLoS Medicine, 2012.

    资助
    Supported by grants from the British Heart Foundation and UK Medical Research Council to the University of Oxford Clinical Trial Service Unit and Epidemiological Studies Unit (CTSU), and the Oxford BHF Centre for Research Excellence (JCH).     结果
    In 19 unpublished datasets (48,175 CHD cases, 67,961 controls), the MTHFR 677TT against CC genotype gave a CHD odds ratio of 1.02 (95% CI 0.98-1.07), and 1.01 (0.95-1.07) in unsupplemented low-folate populations; homocysteine was expected to be about 20% higher with TT than CC.
