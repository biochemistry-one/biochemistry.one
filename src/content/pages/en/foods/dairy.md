## What it is

Milk is mostly water [@fukagawa-2022-fooddata-central].

Per 100 g, whole milk contains 88.1 g water, 4.81 g of the sugar lactose, 3.27 g protein, 3.20 g fat and 123 mg calcium [@fukagawa-2022-fooddata-central].

## What happens in the body

An enzyme in the small intestine, lactase, splits lactose into two sugars, galactose and glucose [@uniprot-2025-knowledgebase].

::pathway{id="lactose-leloir"}

Lactase is encoded by LCT [@uniprot-2025-knowledgebase]. In most humans lactase declines after weaning [@tishkoff-2007-lactase]. It persists in over 90% of Swedes and Danes and in about 1% of Chinese [@tishkoff-2007-lactase]. In Europeans, persistence is mediated by LCT −13910 C>T (rs4988235) [@misselwitz-2019-lactose; @liebert-2017-lactase].

Undigested lactose reaches the colon, where bacteria ferment it to hydrogen, carbon dioxide, methane and short-chain fatty acids [@misselwitz-2019-lactose].

## What studies show

In studies that follow large groups of people, no link between milk and fractures was found in 25 of 28 analyses [@bolland-2015-fracture].

In a Swedish cohort, 61,433 women were followed for a mean 20.1 years [@michaelsson-2014-milk]. Three or more glasses of milk a day, against less than one, was associated with 4.2 against 3.1 hip fractures per 1000 person-years (age standardised; adjusted hazard ratio 1.60, 95% CI 1.39 to 1.84) [@michaelsson-2014-milk]. Deaths were 17.7 against 10.7 per 1000 person-years (1.93, 1.80 to 2.06) [@michaelsson-2014-milk].

In three US cohorts, 217,755 adults were followed for up to 32 years [@ding-2019-dairy-mortality]. The highest against the lowest dairy intake was associated with a hazard ratio for death of 1.07 (1.04 to 1.10) [@ding-2019-dairy-mortality].

In the same US cohorts, total dairy was not associated with type 2 diabetes [@chen-2014-dairy-diabetes]. Each daily serving of yoghurt was associated with a hazard ratio of 0.83 (0.75 to 0.92) [@chen-2014-dairy-diabetes].

The lactase-persistence T allele of rs4988235, a genetic instrument for lifelong milk intake, was associated with lower odds of coronary artery disease (odds ratio 0.86, 0.75 to 0.99; 22,233 cases) [@vimaleswaran-2021-milk-mr].

In a meta-analysis of 32 cohort studies, prostate cancer risk was higher per 400 g of dairy a day (relative risk 1.07, 1.02 to 1.12) [@aune-2015-prostate].

:::industry
A cluster-randomised trial included 7195 residents of 60 Australian aged-care homes [@iuliano-2021-dairy-fractures]. Over a mean 12.6 months, extra milk, yoghurt and cheese reduced fractures from 203 (5.2%) to 121 (3.7%) (hazard ratio 0.67, 0.48 to 0.93) [@iuliano-2021-dairy-fractures]. In a post hoc analysis, the foods reduced hip fractures from 93 (2.4%) to 42 (1.3%) (0.54, 0.35 to 0.83) [@iuliano-2021-dairy-fractures]. The numbers needed to treat were 52 and 82 [@iuliano-2021-dairy-fractures]. Funders included Dairy Australia, Fonterra and the Dutch Dairy Association, and Fonterra donated the foods [@iuliano-2021-dairy-fractures]. The paper states that the funders had no role in the study [@iuliano-2021-dairy-fractures].
:::

:::consensus
A National Institutes of Health consensus panel concluded in 2010 that adults and adolescents with diagnosed lactose malabsorption could take at least 12 g of lactose in one dose with no or minor symptoms [@suchy-2010-lactose-consensus]. That amount is the lactose in a cup of milk [@suchy-2010-lactose-consensus].
:::

:::unresolved
Whether milk itself alters fracture risk or mortality is unsettled [@bolland-2015-fracture; @michaelsson-2014-milk].
:::

## Processing

Cheese-making, fermentation and lactase treatment lower the lactose content of dairy foods [@misselwitz-2019-lactose].

Cheddar contains 0.16 g lactose per 100 g [@fukagawa-2022-fooddata-central]. Yoghurt contains about half the lactose of milk [@misselwitz-2019-lactose]. Live cultures in yoghurt improve digestion of its lactose in people with lactose maldigestion [@efsa-2010-yoghurt]. Of 14 human studies, 13 showed the effect [@efsa-2010-yoghurt].
