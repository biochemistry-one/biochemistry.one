## What it is

Cereal grains consist mostly of starch, made of glucose chains; their outer layers contain most of the fibre [@fukagawa-2022-grains; @nichols-2003-maltase-glucoamylase; @efsa-2010-carbohydrates].

Per 100 g, whole-grain wheat flour contains 57.8 g starch and 10.7 g fibre, white flour 2.7 g fibre [@fukagawa-2022-grains]. Dietary starch is about 25% amylose, a linear α-1,4-glucan, and 75% amylopectin, branched by α-1,6 links [@nichols-2003-maltase-glucoamylase]. Fibre comprises lignin and non-digestible carbohydrates, such as oat β-glucan and retrograded amylose [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates].

## In the body

Digestive enzymes split starch into glucose; colonic bacteria ferment fibre into short-chain fatty acids [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates].

α-Amylase hydrolyses internal α-1,4 links [@nichols-2003-maltase-glucoamylase]. Brush-border maltase-glucoamylase and sucrase-isomaltase release glucose; sucrase-isomaltase supplies almost all α-1,6 activity [@nichols-2003-maltase-glucoamylase].

::pathway{id="starch-digestion"}

Glycaemic index varies with the amylose:amylopectin ratio and with gelatinisation, which makes starch more accessible to digestive enzymes [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates]. Intact grains, parboiled rice and the protein network of pasta lower it [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates].

Colonic bacteria make butyrate from undigested polysaccharides; the acetyl-CoA pathway predominates in stool metagenomes [@vital-2014-butyrate].

::pathway{id="bacterial-butyrate-synthesis"}

Acetate and propionate are absorbed as fuels; butyrate is a main fuel of the colonic mucosa [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates].

In coeliac disease, an immune response to gluten inflames the small intestine of genetically susceptible people [@sollid-1989-hla-dq; @arentz-hansen-2000-deamidation].

Most patients carry HLA-DQ2, encoded by DQA1\*0501 and DQB1\*02; most others carry HLA-DQ8 [@arentz-hansen-2000-deamidation]. Of 94 Norwegian children with the disease, 93 carried DQA1 and DQB1 genes that may encode DQ2, against 14 of 56 controls [@sollid-1989-hla-dq; @arentz-hansen-2000-deamidation]. Tissue transglutaminase, the main coeliac autoantigen, converts selected gliadin glutamines to glutamic acid [@arentz-hansen-2000-deamidation]. Two immunodominant α-gliadin peptides stimulated intestinal T cells only when deamidated, which raised their affinity for DQ2 [@arentz-hansen-2000-deamidation].

## What studies show

In observational studies, people eating the most fibre had less heart disease, diabetes, colorectal cancer and early death [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality].

Highest against lowest fibre intake was associated with an all-cause mortality risk ratio of 0.85 (95% CI 0.79 to 0.91), or 13 fewer deaths per 1000 participants during the studies [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality]. For coronary heart disease incidence, the risk ratio was 0.76 (0.69 to 0.83), 6 fewer cases per 1000 [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality]. For whole grain, the all-cause mortality risk ratio was 0.81 (0.72 to 0.90), 26 fewer deaths per 1000 [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality]. In trials of at least 4 weeks, more fibre lowered body weight by 0.37 kg and total cholesterol by 0.15 mmol/L [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality]. WHO commissioned the work and funded it with five New Zealand bodies; a WHO expert group set its questions and confirmed or modified its certainty ratings [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality].

:::industry
In a meta-analysis funded by General Mills and co-designed by its employee, weight change with whole grain did not differ significantly from control in 9 trials of 12 to 16 weeks (973 adults) [@maki-2019-whole-grain]. For an oat β-glucan claim by CreaNutrition AG, an unpublished meta-analysis it submitted reported LDL cholesterol −0.28 mmol/L in 18 studies of 2 to 12 weeks [@efsa-2010-oat-beta-glucan]. EFSA accepted the effect and could have done so without the proprietary studies [@efsa-2010-oat-beta-glucan].
:::

:::consensus
EFSA considers 25 g of fibre a day adequate for normal laxation in adults [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates]. The European Society for the Study of Coeliac Disease recommends IgA anti-transglutaminase with total IgA as the first test, on a gluten-containing diet [@al-toma-2025-esscd].
:::

:::unresolved
The pooled trials measured only risk factors; disease outcomes came from observational studies [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality]. Independent pooling of 11 whole-grain trials found 0.62 kg lower weight; only 2 of them were in the General Mills-funded analysis [@reynolds-2019-carbohydrate-quality; @maki-2019-whole-grain]. In 26 adults, peak 2-hour rises in interstitial glucose after starch-matched rolls with 43.5% or 27.2% amylose were 1.73 and 1.79 mmol/L (P = 0.514) [@corrado-2025-amylose].
:::
