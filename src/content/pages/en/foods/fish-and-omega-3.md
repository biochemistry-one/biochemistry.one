## What it is

Oily fish supply two long omega-3 fats, EPA and DHA; some plant oils supply a shorter one, α-linolenic acid (ALA) [@fukagawa-2022-salmon; @efsa-2014-seafood].

Per 100 g, raw farmed Atlantic salmon contains 0.862 g eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA, 20:5 n-3) and 1.1 g docosahexaenoic acid (DHA, 22:6 n-3), and cold-pressed linseed oil 53.4 g ALA (18:3 n-3) [@fukagawa-2022-salmon]. Fish are the main dietary source of methylmercury; predatory species contain the most mercury [@efsa-2012-mercury].

## In the body

The body converts only small amounts of ALA to EPA and DHA [@efsa-2010-dietary-fats].

Humans lack Δ12- and Δ15-desaturases, so ALA is essential [@efsa-2010-dietary-fats]. FADS2 catalyses the first, rate-limiting Δ6-desaturation; ELOVL5 and FADS1 complete EPA, and ELOVL2, FADS2 and one peroxisomal β-oxidation cycle convert EPA to DHA [@uniprot-2025-omega-3].

::pathway{id="alpha-linolenic-acid-to-dha"}

Estimated conversion is 8–12% to EPA and possibly under 1% to DHA; dietary linoleic acid, converted by the same enzymes, lowers it [@efsa-2010-dietary-fats]. Homozygotes for the derived FADS haplotype D had 24% more plasma DHA than those for haplotype A [@ameur-2012-fads-haplotype].

Methylmercury crosses the placenta and blood–brain barrier; EFSA derived its tolerable weekly intake, 1.3 µg/kg body weight, from prenatal neurodevelopment, and high fish consumers may exceed it up to about sixfold [@efsa-2012-mercury].

## What studies show

In independently funded research, fish-oil capsules did not reduce heart attacks and strokes taken together [@manson-2019-vital-omega-3; @aung-2018-omega-3].

VITAL gave 25,871 US adults without cardiovascular disease 1 g fish oil daily (460 mg EPA, 380 mg DHA) or placebo; over a median 5.3 years, major cardiovascular events occurred in 3.0% against 3.2% (hazard ratio 0.92, 95% CI 0.80 to 1.06) [@manson-2019-vital-omega-3].

In a meta-analysis of 10 trials in 77,917 high-risk people (mean 4.4 years), the rate ratio for major vascular events was 0.97 (0.93 to 1.01) [@aung-2018-omega-3].

:::industry
ASCEND, whose capsule suppliers funded packaging and commented on its design and manuscript, gave 15,480 UK adults with diabetes the same dose or olive oil [@bowman-2018-ascend]. Over a mean 7.4 years, serious vascular events occurred in 8.9% against 9.2% (rate ratio 0.97, 0.87 to 1.08) [@bowman-2018-ascend].

REDUCE-IT, sponsored and analysed by Amarin, gave 8179 statin-treated patients with raised triglycerides 4 g daily of icosapent ethyl (EPA ethyl ester) or a mineral-oil placebo [@bhatt-2019-reduce-it]. Over a median 4.9 years, the primary composite occurred in 17.2% against 22.0% (hazard ratio 0.75, 0.68 to 0.83), a difference of 4.8 percentage points (number needed to treat 21), and hospitalisation for atrial fibrillation or flutter in 3.1% against 2.1% [@bhatt-2019-reduce-it].

STRENGTH, funded by AstraZeneca, compared 4 g daily of EPA and DHA carboxylic acids with corn oil in 13,078 statin-treated patients; it was stopped for futility at a median 42.0 months [@nicholls-2020-strength]. Primary events occurred in 12.0% against 12.2% (hazard ratio 0.99, 0.90 to 1.09), and new atrial fibrillation in 2.2% against 1.3% (number needed to harm 114) [@nicholls-2020-strength].
:::

:::consensus
EFSA links 1–2 seafood servings a week, up to 3–4 in pregnancy, with better child neurodevelopment and lower adult coronary mortality [@efsa-2014-seafood]. NICE advises against omega-3 compounds to prevent cardiovascular disease, except icosapent ethyl with a statin in established cardiovascular disease, fasting triglycerides ≥1.7 mmol/l and LDL cholesterol >1.04 and ≤2.60 mmol/l [@nice-2023-lipid-modification; @nice-2022-icosapent-ethyl].
:::

:::unresolved
NICE judged the REDUCE-IT effect uncertain because the mineral-oil placebo may have caused harm [@nice-2022-icosapent-ethyl]. LDL cholesterol rose 10.2% with mineral oil and 3.1% with icosapent ethyl, a difference the authors judge unlikely to explain the 25% lower risk [@bhatt-2019-reduce-it]. STRENGTH's authors cite a rise of over 30% in C-reactive protein with mineral oil, unlike corn oil [@nicholls-2020-strength].
:::
