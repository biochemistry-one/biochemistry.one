## What it is

Olive oil is the fat pressed from olives; most of its fatty acids have one double bond, and virgin oils contain small amounts of phenolic compounds [@fukagawa-2022-olive-oil; @neveu-2010-phenol-explorer].

Per 100 g, extra virgin olive oil contains on average 67.8 g cis-18:1 fatty acids, 15.4 g saturated fatty acids and 8.4 g linoleic acid [@fukagawa-2022-olive-oil]. Its main phenolic compounds are the dialdehydes of oleuropein aglycone, mean 25.16 mg per 100 g (range 0.08 to 94.20), and of ligstroside aglycone, 14.28 mg, against 0.77 mg of free hydroxytyrosol [@neveu-2010-phenol-explorer].

## In the body

Human cells make oleic acid from a saturated fatty acid, and the gut probably absorbs more than half of olive oil's phenolic compounds [@uniprot-2025-knowledgebase; @efsa-2011-olive-polyphenols].

Stearoyl-CoA desaturase (SCD) forms oleoyl-CoA by inserting a cis Δ9 double bond into stearoyl-CoA, with O2 and electrons from reduced cytochrome b5 [@uniprot-2025-knowledgebase].

Absorbed tyrosol and hydroxytyrosol enter lipoprotein fractions, and hydroxytyrosol is excreted in urine as a glucuronide [@efsa-2011-olive-polyphenols].

## What studies show

In a Spanish trial, a Mediterranean diet with extra virgin olive oil was followed by fewer heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths than low-fat advice [@estruch-2018-predimed].

PREDIMED assigned 7447 adults at high cardiovascular risk to a Mediterranean diet with extra virgin olive oil, 1 litre a week per household, a Mediterranean diet with nuts, or advice to reduce dietary fat [@estruch-2018-predimed]. Over a median 4.8 years, myocardial infarction, stroke or cardiovascular death occurred in 96 of 2543 participants on the olive oil diet and 109 of 2450 controls [@estruch-2018-predimed]. The 5-year risks were 3.6% and 5.7% (adjusted hazard ratio 0.69, 95% CI 0.53 to 0.91), a number needed to treat of 48 for 5 years [@estruch-2018-predimed]. Deaths from any cause numbered 118 and 114 (hazard ratio 0.90, 0.69 to 1.18) [@estruch-2018-predimed].

The 2013 report was withdrawn after deviations from randomisation emerged, including 425 household members and 467 participants at one site who were not randomised individually [@estruch-2018-predimed]. After exclusion of 1588 participants with known or suspected deviations, the olive oil hazard ratio was 0.71 (0.52 to 0.97) [@estruch-2018-predimed]. Public bodies, chiefly Spain's Instituto de Salud Carlos III, and the Fundación Mapfre funded the trial [@estruch-2018-predimed]. Olive oil and nut firms and trade bodies donated the foods; sponsors had no role in design, analysis or reporting [@estruch-2018-predimed].

:::industry
In a crossover trial in 200 healthy European men, olive oils with 2.7, 164 or 366 mg/kg of phenolics, 25 mL a day for 3 weeks each, changed oxidised LDL by 1.21, −1.48 and −3.21 U/L [@covas-2006-eurolive]. Only the last 95% CI, −5.1 to −0.8, excluded zero [@covas-2006-eurolive]. The European Commission funded the trial; two authors listed at Oy Jurilab, the company that measured the oxidation markers, took part in its design and analysis [@covas-2006-eurolive].
:::

:::consensus
In 2011 the EFSA nutrition panel concluded, from that trial and smaller ones, that olive oil polyphenols protect LDL particles from oxidative damage at 5 mg of hydroxytyrosol and its derivatives a day [@efsa-2011-olive-polyphenols]. Some olive oils may supply too little of them within a balanced diet [@efsa-2011-olive-polyphenols].
:::

:::unresolved
PREDIMED tested a dietary pattern; fish and legume intake also rose against control [@estruch-2018-predimed]. The 2013 report, retracted in 2018, judged the oil and nuts probably responsible for most of the benefit [@estruch-2013-predimed; @estruch-2018-retraction]. The 2018 report states that the largest between-group differences involved them [@estruch-2018-predimed].

In enzyme assays, oleocanthal, the deacetoxy-dialdehydic ligstroside aglycone, inhibited COX-1 and COX-2, as ibuprofen does (IC50 23 and 28 µM) [@beauchamp-2005-oleocanthal]. The funding statement of that study could not be read [@beauchamp-2005-oleocanthal].
:::
