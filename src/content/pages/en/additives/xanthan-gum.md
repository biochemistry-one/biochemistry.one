## What it is

Bacteria make xanthan gum, a thickener in sauces, baked goods, gluten-free foods and food for dysphagia [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum].

*Xanthomonas campestris* secretes it in pure culture [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum; @ostrowski-2022-xanthan]. Every second glucose of its β-(1→4) backbone bears a partly acetylated and pyruvylated mannose–glucuronic acid–mannose side chain [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum]. It is E 415 in the EU, INS 415 in Codex [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum; @jecfa-1999-xanthan-gum].

## How often it is used

Few additives occur in more products [@chazelas-2020-additives].

In 2019, 7327 of 126,556 French products contained xanthan gum, sixth among additives [@chazelas-2020-additives]. Of 106,489 NutriNet-Santé adults, 2009 to 2020, 79.74% consumed it, mean intake 439.26 mg/day [@chazelas-2021-mixtures].

## In the body

Human enzymes leave xanthan gum intact; gut bacteria of some people break it down [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum; @ostrowski-2022-xanthan].

Too large to absorb, it passes to the colon [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum]. Of faecal cultures from 60 adults, half degraded it; the uncultured Ruminococcaceae R.UCG13 was common to all 20 analysed [@ostrowski-2022-xanthan]. Its locus occurred in every industrialised population sampled, in none from Yanomami, Hadza or Burkina Faso [@ostrowski-2022-xanthan]. *Bacteroides intestinalis* grew on the released oligosaccharides, not the polymer [@ostrowski-2022-xanthan].

## What studies show

In gram doses it bulks the stool [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum].

Five men took 10.4 to 12.9 g/day for 23 days: faecal weight rose, serum cholesterol fell 10% [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum]. Eighteen men took 15 g/day for 10 days: stool output and flatulence rose, some reporting abdominal discomfort [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum].

After feeds thickened with a xanthan gum product, 22 infants, 21 preterm, developed necrotising enterocolitis; 7 died [@younes-2023-xanthan-gum].

In rats fed 925 mg/kg of diet for 70 days, colonic IL-1β was 18.29 against 11.41 pg/mg protein [@silva-rischiteli-2026-colon].

Among 104,139 adults with 1056 type 2 diabetes cases over a mean 6.8 years, the hazard ratio was 1.08 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.14) per 500 mg/day, absolute risks unreported [@salame-2024-diabetes].

:::industry
Manufacturers submitted the piglet studies behind the reference point, 750 mg/kg body weight per day [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum; @younes-2023-xanthan-gum].
:::

:::consensus
For infants under 16 weeks on special formulae at 1200 mg/L, high-level exposure was 312 mg/kg body weight per day, a margin of 2.4 judged sufficient [@younes-2023-xanthan-gum].
:::

:::unresolved
Funding of the feeding studies is undetermined [@eastwood-1987-xanthan; @daly-1993-xanthan; @osilesi-1985-xanthan]. Assessors judged the enterocolitis cases unrelated to additive use in formula manufacture, the doses unknown [@younes-2023-xanthan-gum; @beal-2012-enterocolitis]. Causal attribution is untested, also for a mixture containing xanthan gum [@salame-2024-diabetes; @payen-de-la-garanderie-2025-mixtures]. Consequences of R.UCG13 expansion are unmeasured [@ostrowski-2022-xanthan].
:::

## Regulation

No regulator sets a daily limit [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum; @fda-2026-xanthan-gum; @jecfa-1999-xanthan-gum].

The European Union authorises E 415 at quantum satis in most categories, with no numerical acceptable daily intake [@mortensen-2017-xanthan-gum]. The United States permits use under good manufacturing practice; Japan lists it among existing additives [@fda-2026-xanthan-gum; @jfcrf-2025-existing-additives].
