## What it is

Sodium carbonates are alkaline sodium salts used as raising agents and acidity regulators; baking soda is one [@jecfa-2003-sodium-carbonate; @jecfa-2003-sodium-hydrogen-carbonate; @nhc-2024-gb2760].

E 500 comprises sodium carbonate, Na₂CO₃ (i); sodium hydrogen carbonate, NaHCO₃ (ii); and sodium sesquicarbonate, Na₂CO₃·NaHCO₃·2H₂O (iii); the INS numbers are the same [@ec-2012-specifications; @jecfa-2003-sodium-carbonate; @jecfa-2003-sodium-hydrogen-carbonate; @jecfa-2003-sodium-sesquicarbonate]. Sodium carbonate, diphosphates and lecithins occurred together in 1,667 French products, mostly biscuits [@chazelas-2020-additives].

## How often it is used

In 2019, 8,587 of 126,556 French packaged foods contained E 500, fourth among additives [@chazelas-2020-additives].

Of 106,489 NutriNet-Santé adults in 2009–2020, 67.10% consumed it; mean intake was 1267.46 mg/day, 95th percentile 4950 mg/day [@chazelas-2021-mixtures].

## In the body

Baking soda neutralises stomach acid, and the kidneys regulate the body's sodium and bicarbonate [@fda-2026-antacids; @palmer-2015-nephron; @skelton-2010-proximal].

The proximal tubule reclaims about 80% of filtered bicarbonate [@skelton-2010-proximal].

## What studies show

Bicarbonate tablets did not improve physical function in older people with advanced kidney disease [@bicarb-2020-kidney].

BiCARB randomised 300 adults aged 60 or over, with eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m² and serum bicarbonate below 22 mmol/L, to up to 3 g/day or placebo; mean follow-up was 16.4 months [@bicarb-2020-kidney]. The 12-month physical performance score differed by −0.4 points with bicarbonate (95% CI −0.9 to 0.1) [@bicarb-2020-kidney]. 131 of 152 participants on bicarbonate had 457 adverse events; 132 of 148 on placebo had 400 [@bicarb-2020-kidney].

JECFA's acceptable daily intake is "not limited" for sodium carbonate and hydrogen carbonate, provided cation intake is considered [@jecfa-1967-carbonates; @jecfa-2003-sodium-carbonate; @jecfa-2003-sodium-hydrogen-carbonate].

:::industry
In a study partly funded by Church and Dwight, 7 days of 240 mmol/day sodium chloride raised blood pressure by 16/8 mm Hg in 5 salt-sensitive hypertensive men; equimolar sodium citrate did not [@kurtz-1987-anion].
:::

:::consensus
WHO strongly recommends under 2 g sodium a day for adults [@who-2012-sodium]. Citing rat studies, proposed mechanisms and few clinical observations, EFSA noted evidence that chloride can contribute to the blood-pressure effect of sodium chloride [@turck-2019-sodium].
:::

:::unresolved
In a week-long trial of undetermined funding, sodium bicarbonate mineral water lowered systolic pressure by 5 mm Hg in 10 hypertensive adults; sodium chloride did not [@luft-1990-bicarbonate]. The NutriNet-Santé investigators judge human evidence on regular additive intake lacking [@chazelas-2021-mixtures]. In a case series of undetermined funding, 12 of 192 symptomatic baking-soda misusers were admitted with electrolyte imbalance, metabolic alkalosis or respiratory depression [@al-abri-2014-baking-soda].
:::

## Regulation

All four jurisdictions permit sodium carbonate and baking soda [@ec-2011-additives; @fda-2026-sodium-carbonates; @ffcr-2024-designated-additives; @nhc-2024-gb2760].

| Jurisdiction | Rule |
|---|---|
| European Union | Group I, quantum satis [@ec-2011-additives] |
| United States | Generally recognised as safe; sesquicarbonate only in cream [@fda-2026-sodium-carbonates] |
| Japan | Sodium carbonate and bicarbonate designated [@ffcr-2024-designated-additives] |
| China | Carbonate and bicarbonate as needed outside listed foods; sesquicarbonate only in pastries, biscuits and goat milk [@nhc-2024-gb2760] |
