## What these molecules are

Most of 95,442 French adults ate lecithins (E322), mono- and diglycerides (E471) and carrageenan (E407), but few ate carboxymethylcellulose (E466) or polysorbate 80 (E433) [@sellem-2023-cardiovascular].

## What digestion does to them

Digestive enzymes break down mono- and diglycerides and lecithins, but carboxymethylcellulose reaches the colon intact [@younes-2017-glycerides; @mortensen-2017-lecithins; @younes-2018-celluloses]. Faeces contain 98 to 100% of ingested carrageenan in several species and 90 to 97% of the polyoxyethylene moiety of polysorbate 80 in volunteers [@younes-2018-carrageenan; @efsa-2015-polysorbates].

## The barrier at issue

In mouse colon, the inner layer of mucus is free of bacteria [@johansson-2008-mucus].

## What human trials measured

In healthy adults, 15 g carboxymethylcellulose per day for 11 days altered gut bacteria without changing inflammation markers [@chassaing-2022-carboxymethylcellulose]. Bacteria reached the inner mucus layer in 2 of 7 participants against 0 of 9 controls (P = 0.175) [@chassaing-2022-carboxymethylcellulose]. In Crohn's disease, remission after 4 weeks did not differ between high- and low-emulsifier diets (9 of 12 and 7 of 12, P = 0.67) [@fitzpatrick-2025-crohns]. In ulcerative colitis, relapse within one year occurred in 3 of 5 patients given carrageenan and 0 of 7 given placebo (log-rank P = 0.046) [@bhattacharyya-2017-carrageenan].

## What the cohorts found

In French cohorts, some emulsifiers were linked to small increases in cardiovascular disease, cancer and diabetes [@sellem-2023-cardiovascular; @sellem-2024-cancer; @salame-2024-diabetes]. Over a median 7.4 years, hazard ratios for cardiovascular disease per standard deviation of intake were 1.03 (95% CI 1.01 to 1.05) for carboxymethylcellulose and 1.07 (1.04 to 1.11) for total mono- and diglycerides [@sellem-2023-cardiovascular]. Over a mean 6.7 years, modelled absolute risk of cancer by age 60 was 11.2%, 11.8% and 12.4% across E471 intake categories [@sellem-2024-cancer]. The highest third of intake had a cancer hazard ratio of 1.15 (1.04 to 1.27) [@sellem-2024-cancer]. Over a mean 6.8 years, the hazard ratio for type 2 diabetes was 1.03 (1.01 to 1.05) per 100 mg of carrageenan per day [@salame-2024-diabetes].

## Animal and laboratory evidence

In mice, carboxymethylcellulose or polysorbate 80 at 1.0% in drinking water for 12 weeks more than halved the distance between gut bacteria and the gut lining [@chassaing-2015-emulsifiers]. The mice developed low-grade inflammation [@chassaing-2015-emulsifiers]. Germ-free mice were unaffected [@chassaing-2015-emulsifiers]. The 1.0% dose is far above human polysorbate exposure [@efsa-2015-polysorbates].

## Regulatory limits

European assessors set daily limits only for polysorbates, at 25 mg per kg body weight, and carrageenan, at a temporary 75 mg [@efsa-2015-polysorbates; @younes-2018-carrageenan; @younes-2018-celluloses; @younes-2017-glycerides; @mortensen-2017-lecithins]. Brand-loyal exposure to carrageenan at the 95th percentile was up to about tenfold higher [@younes-2018-carrageenan].

:::consensus
The International Organization for the Study of Inflammatory Bowel Diseases judged it may be prudent for patients with Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis to reduce processed foods containing carrageenan, carboxymethylcellulose and polysorbate 80, on very low evidence [@levine-2020-ioibd].
:::

:::industry
According to a review funded by FMC Corporation, a carrageenan manufacturer, carrageenan is not hydrolysed to poligeenan under conditions in the stomach [@mckim-2019-poligeenan].
:::

:::unresolved
In 22 healthy adults, the unstressed urinary lactulose:rhamnose ratio was 0.030 on a high-emulsifier diet and 0.042 on a low-emulsifier diet (P = 0.04) [@fitzpatrick-2024-barrier]. After corticotrophin-releasing hormone, it was raised by 89% and lowered by 39%, respectively (P = 0.001 between diets) [@fitzpatrick-2024-barrier]. In a trial of undetermined funding in 60 healthy adults, carrageenan raised transcellular permeability from baseline (P = 0.04) [@wellens-2026-emulsifiers].
:::
