## What it is

Modified starches are starches altered chemically to thicken, gel, stabilise or emulsify foods such as desserts and mayonnaise [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches; @fscj-2007-modified-starches].

The EU authorises twelve, E1404 to E1452, made by oxidation, phosphorylation, cross-linking, acetylation, hydroxypropylation or octenyl succination, alone or combined; E1404 to E1451 have identical INS numbers [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches].

## How often it is used

Modified starches are the third commonest additive in French food products, listed on 10,100 of 126,556 in 2019 [@chazelas-2020-additives].

Individual starches are undercounted, since "modified starch" alone may be declared on labels [@chazelas-2020-additives; @mortensen-2017-modified-starches]. Of NutriNet-Santé adults, 91.48% consumed them, a mean 1596.45 mg/day or 24.33 mg/kg body weight/day, against 112.0 in EFSA's French non-brand-loyal scenario [@chazelas-2021-mixtures].

## In the body

Modified starches are not absorbed intact: digestive enzymes split them, and colonic bacteria ferment the rest [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches].

EFSA's fibre definition lists chemically modified starches under resistant starch [@efsa-2010-carbohydrates]. Fermentation yields acetate, propionate and butyrate, the main colonocyte fuel, formed mainly via the acetyl-CoA pathway [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches; @vital-2014-butyrate].

## What studies show

In a French cohort, people eating more of a mixture of modified starches, pectin, guar gum, carrageenan, polyphosphates, potassium sorbate, curcumin and xanthan gum developed type 2 diabetes slightly more often [@payen-de-la-garanderie-2025-mixtures].

Over a mean 7.7 years, the hazard ratio was 1.08 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.15) per standard deviation; 465 of 36,214 in the highest third and 260 of 36,215 in the lowest developed it [@payen-de-la-garanderie-2025-mixtures]. Rats fed up to 31,000 mg/kg body weight/day showed no effects relevant to humans; adults tolerated one 25,000 mg dose of E1450 [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches].

:::industry
In a 15-adult crossover trial sponsored by MGP Ingredients, maker of the cross-linked phosphorylated wheat starch tested, with an MGP co-author, the primary 0–2 h incremental glucose area was 106 ± 16 mmol·min/L, against 124 ± 16 with control cookies (mean ± SEM; P = 0.087) [@wolever-2023-rs4].
:::

:::unresolved
Causality cannot be established; adjusted for modified-starch intake, the mixture hazard ratio was 1.06 (1.00 to 1.12) [@payen-de-la-garanderie-2025-mixtures]. Infant faecal cultures fermented maize hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate with more gas, a faster pH fall and a less varied microbiota than locust bean gum; funding is undetermined [@gonzalez-bermudez-2018-thickeners]. EFSA assessed six infant trials of E1450, all industry submissions not designed to test it [@younes-2020-octenyl-succinate].
:::

## Regulation

The EU and the United States permit modified starches; EFSA, and Japan's Food Safety Commission for all but E1452, set no numerical acceptable daily intake [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches; @fda-2026-food-starch-modified; @fscj-2007-modified-starches].

EFSA's refined brand-loyal 95th-percentile exposure estimate was 3,053 mg/kg body weight/day, in toddlers [@mortensen-2017-modified-starches]. For E1450, EFSA found no indication of concern in infants below 16 weeks up to 2,725 mg/kg body weight/day [@younes-2020-octenyl-succinate]. China lists ten, not E1451 or E1452, mostly for use as production requires [@nhc-2024-gb2760].
