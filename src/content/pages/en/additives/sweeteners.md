## What they are

Sweeteners are food additives that taste sweet, some of them 200 to 500 times more intensely than sugar [@castle-2024-saccharin; @castle-2025-acesulfame-k; @jecfa-2023-aspartame].

Aspartame is L-aspartyl-L-phenylalanine methyl ester, sucralose a trichlorinated sucrose derivative, erythritol a four-carbon polyol, and steviol glycosides constituents of *Stevia rebaudiana* leaves [@efsa-2013-aspartame; @castle-2026-sucralose; @younes-2023-erythritol; @castle-2024-steviol-glycosides].

The sweet receptor is a TAS1R2–TAS1R3 heterodimer; sucralose and aspartame bind the same pocket of its TAS1R2 subunit [@juen-2025-sweetness].

## In the body

Most sweeteners are excreted largely unchanged; aspartame is hydrolysed to two amino acids and methanol [@castle-2025-acesulfame-k; @castle-2024-saccharin; @castle-2026-sucralose; @younes-2023-erythritol; @efsa-2013-aspartame].

Intestinal esterases and peptidases, including aminopeptidase A, hydrolyse aspartame with a half-life of minutes, yielding up to 10% methanol by weight [@efsa-2013-aspartame].

::pathway{id="aspartame-hydrolysis"}

In phenylketonuria, an autosomal recessive deficiency of hepatic phenylalanine hydroxylase, phenylalanine from food and aspartame accumulates and, untreated, causes intellectual impairment [@efsa-2013-aspartame].

Acesulfame K, saccharin and, within 24 hours, 78 to 92% of erythritol are excreted unchanged in urine [@castle-2025-acesulfame-k; @castle-2024-saccharin; @younes-2023-erythritol]. Only 8.8 to 21.7% of sucralose is absorbed [@castle-2026-sucralose]. Colonic bacteria release steviol from steviol glycosides; steviol is excreted as glucuronide, mainly in urine [@castle-2024-steviol-glycosides].

## What studies show

In trials, sugar-free drinks slowed children's weight gain, and replacing them with water did not lower blood glucose in type 2 diabetes [@de-ruyter-2012-drink; @odegaard-2026-sodas].

Over 18 months, 641 children given a daily sugar-free or sugar-sweetened drink gained 6.35 and 7.37 kg (difference −1.01 kg, 95% CI −1.54 to −0.48) [@de-ruyter-2012-drink]. In 181 adults with type 2 diabetes, HbA1c over 24 weeks rose from 7.20% to 7.44% with water and went from 7.19% to 7.14% with habitual sweetened beverages (adjusted difference 0.29 percentage points, P = 0.013) [@odegaard-2026-sodas]. Without randomisation, 30 g erythritol raised plasma erythritol over 1000-fold and, unlike 30 g glucose, enhanced platelet aggregation in all 10 volunteers [@witkowski-2024-platelets].

:::industry
In SWITCH, funded by the American Beverage Association, which could review the manuscript, 52-week weight loss was 1.4 kg greater with sweetened drinks than with water in completers (90% CI 0.2 to 2.6) and 0.8 kg (−0.1 to 1.7) with imputation for all 493 [@harrold-2024-switch].

In three cardiac cohorts, one partly funded by Sanofi, top-quartile against bottom-quartile plasma erythritol had adjusted hazard ratios of 1.80 to 2.95 for 3-year death, myocardial infarction or stroke; in vitro, erythritol enhanced platelet activation [@witkowski-2023-erythritol].
:::

:::consensus
WHO suggests that non-sugar sweeteners not be used for weight control or disease prevention (conditional, low certainty) [@who-2023-sweeteners]. Its review found 0.71 kg lower weight in trials mostly of 3 months or less, unchanged fasting glucose, and cohort hazard ratios, over an average 13 years, of 1.23 to 1.34 for type 2 diabetes and 1.32 for cardiovascular disease [@who-2023-sweeteners]. IARC classified aspartame as possibly carcinogenic (Group 2B, a hazard rather than risk rating) on limited human and animal evidence [@iarc-2024-aspartame].
:::

:::unresolved
IARC could not exclude bias or confounding in the liver cancer cohorts; JECFA found the human cancer evidence unconvincing [@iarc-2024-aspartame; @jecfa-2023-aspartame]. EFSA judged a causal role of dietary erythritol in cardiovascular disease undemonstrated [@younes-2023-erythritol].
:::

EFSA acceptable daily intakes, mg/kg body weight:

| Sweetener | Intake |
|---|---|
| Aspartame | 40 [@efsa-2013-aspartame] |
| Acesulfame K | 15 [@castle-2025-acesulfame-k] |
| Saccharin | 9, as free imide [@castle-2024-saccharin] |
| Sucralose | 15 [@castle-2026-sucralose] |
| Steviol glycosides | 4, as steviol [@castle-2024-steviol-glycosides] |
| Erythritol | 500; 95th-percentile estimates reach or exceed it [@younes-2023-erythritol] |

## Regulation

In 2023 JECFA, a United Nations expert committee, kept the aspartame limit at 40 mg per kg body weight a day, above estimated intakes [@jecfa-2023-aspartame].

The limit does not apply in phenylketonuria, and EU labels must declare aspartame a source of phenylalanine [@efsa-2013-aspartame].
