## Folate forms

Humans must obtain [folate](/foods/folate/) from food [@ducker-2017-one-carbon]. Cells use it to hold single carbon atoms for making DNA bases and the amino acid methionine [@ducker-2017-one-carbon].

Folic acid, the synthetic form added to foods and supplements, becomes active only after dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR) reduces it to tetrahydrofolate (THF) [@turck-2023-folate; @ducker-2017-one-carbon].

## Thymidylate and purines

Dividing cells use single carbon atoms to make the DNA base thymine and the purine bases [@ducker-2017-one-carbon].

Thymidylate synthase transfers the carbon of 5,10-methylene-THF to dUMP, forming dTMP and dihydrofolate (DHF) [@ducker-2017-one-carbon].

::pathway{id="thymidylate-cycle"}

[Methotrexate](/medicines/methotrexate/) blocks DHFR and traps folate as DHF [@ducker-2017-one-carbon].

## MTHFR and the 677C>T variant

The enzyme MTHFR converts part of the folate pool into methyl-folate, which supplies methyl groups for methionine [@yamada-2001-mthfr]. A common variant of its gene, 677C>T, makes the enzyme less stable [@yamada-2001-mthfr].

Cell extracts from TT homozygotes have about 30% of the activity of CC extracts [@yamada-2001-mthfr]. About 10–15% of people of European origin and of Japanese are TT homozygotes [@yamada-2001-mthfr]. The variant is the most frequent genetic cause of mild hyperhomocysteinaemia [@yamada-2001-mthfr].

## Methionine synthase and vitamin B12

Methionine synthase requires [vitamin B12](/foods/vitamin-b12/) [@gherasim-2013-cobalamin]. It moves the methyl group from methyl-folate to homocysteine, which regenerates methionine and frees folate [@gherasim-2013-cobalamin; @turck-2023-folate].

::pathway{id="folate-cycle"}

When B12 is lacking, methyl-folate accumulates, because no other reaction consumes it and the MTHFR reaction does not reverse [@turck-2023-folate]. This methyl-folate trap produces a functional folate deficiency [@turck-2023-folate; @blount-1997-uracil].

## S-adenosylmethionine and homocysteine

Methionine is converted into S-adenosylmethionine, the main donor of methyl groups [@lu-2012-adomet]. Each methyl transfer leaves homocysteine, which is converted back to methionine or broken down to cysteine [@lu-2012-adomet].

::pathway{id="one-carbon"}

In 15 randomised trials with 71,422 participants followed for 1 to 7.3 years, lowering homocysteine with vitamins B6, B9 or B12 did not prevent myocardial infarction [@marti-carvajal-2017-homocysteine]. It occurred in 7.1% of treated and 6.0% of placebo participants (risk ratio 1.02, 95% CI 0.95 to 1.10) [@marti-carvajal-2017-homocysteine]. Stroke occurred in 4.3% and 5.1% (0.90, 0.82 to 0.99), a crude absolute difference of 0.8 percentage points and a number needed to treat of about 125 [@marti-carvajal-2017-homocysteine].

## Megaloblastic anaemia

When folate or B12 is lacking, the bone marrow cannot make DNA properly, and the blood contains too few red cells, which are larger than normal [@ducker-2017-one-carbon; @turck-2023-folate].

Low 5,10-methylene-THF slows thymidylate synthesis, so DNA polymerase incorporates uracil [@blount-1997-uracil]. In folate-deficient adults (n = 8), blood DNA contained eight times as many uracils as in controls (n = 14) [@blount-1997-uracil]. B12 deficiency can also cause a myeloneuropathy through demyelination, which is almost never seen in folate deficiency [@turck-2023-folate].

:::industry
In the Diabetes Prevention Program, partly funded by Bristol-Myers Squibb and Parke-Davis, adults were assigned [metformin](/medicines/metformin/) 850 mg twice daily or placebo [@aroda-2016-metformin]. After a mean 5 years, serum B12 was at or below 203 pg/ml in 37 of 859 (4.3%) and 20 of 856 (2.3%), respectively [@aroda-2016-metformin]. The absolute difference was 2.0 percentage points, and the number needed to harm about 51 [@aroda-2016-metformin].
:::

:::consensus
The European Food Safety Authority set an Adequate Intake of 4 µg cobalamin per day for adults [@efsa-2015-cobalamin]. It retained a tolerable upper intake level of 1000 µg folic acid per day for adults, because folic acid can correct the anaemia of B12 deficiency while neurological damage progresses [@turck-2023-folate].
:::

:::unresolved
Whether moderately raised homocysteine causes vascular disease is disputed [@clarke-2012-mthfr; @holmes-2011-mthfr-stroke]. In 19 unpublished datasets, the *MTHFR* 677TT genotype gave a coronary odds ratio of 1.02 (95% CI 0.98 to 1.07) against CC [@clarke-2012-mthfr]. In 86 published studies, it was 1.15 (1.09 to 1.21) [@clarke-2012-mthfr]. The authors attribute the discrepancy to publication bias or methodological problems [@clarke-2012-mthfr]. In 237 genetic datasets, the TT–CC difference in homocysteine was 3.12 µmol/L (2.23 to 4.01) in Asia and 0.13 µmol/L (−0.85 to 1.11) in folate-fortified regions [@holmes-2011-mthfr-stroke]. The stroke odds ratios were 1.68 (1.44 to 1.97) and 1.03 (0.84 to 1.25) [@holmes-2011-mthfr-stroke]. No trial had tested homocysteine lowering exclusively in a low-folate region [@holmes-2011-mthfr-stroke].
:::
