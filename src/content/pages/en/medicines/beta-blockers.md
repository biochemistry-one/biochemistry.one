summary: Beta-blockers can slow the heart dangerously, narrow the airways, mask and prolong low blood sugar, lower a newborn's blood sugar, raise deaths when started before surgery and provoke heart attacks when stopped suddenly. Their receptors are also found in airway muscle and fat tissue, and blockade is linked with new diabetes, higher blood fats, gout, fatigue, low mood and disturbed sleep. In manufacturer-supported trials in heart failure with a weak heart, one death was avoided per 18 to 28 people treated over 1 to 1.3 years; after a heart attack with normal pumping, serious events were recorded in 8.1% with the drug and 8.3% without over 3.6 years in independent trials.

## Serious harms

Beta-blockers are prescribed for high blood pressure, angina, heart failure and after myocardial infarction [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2026-carvedilol-label].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bradycardia, heart block, cardiac arrest | Bradycardia 6.6% against 2.4% of 8351 surgical patients; 13% on sotalol | With verapamil or diltiazem | [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2024-sotalol-label; @fda-2026-atenolol-label] |
| Torsade de pointes | Boxed warning for sotalol | Renal impairment, women, higher dose | [@fda-2024-sotalol-label] |
| Worse heart failure; hypotension | Hypotension 20.2% against 12.6% on carvedilol after infarction | Up-titration; also without previous failure | [@fda-2026-atenolol-label; @fda-2026-carvedilol-label] |
| Infarction or ventricular arrhythmia after abrupt stopping | Label warning, not boxed | Coronary disease, even unrecognised | [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2026-atenolol-label] |
| Severe, prolonged or masked hypoglycaemia | Label warning, any time in treatment | Diabetes, children, fasting; infants on propranolol, exceptionally as seizures or coma | [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @ema-2026-hemangiol] |
| Neonatal hypoglycaemia and bradycardia; small for gestational age | 4.3% and 1.6% of 10,585 exposed infants, against 1.2% and 0.5% | Exposure at delivery; atenolol from the second trimester | [@bateman-2016-neonatal; @fda-2026-atenolol-label] |
| Bronchospasm in asthma | FEV1 fall of 20% or more in 1 in 3 on non-selective eye drops | Asthma | [@morales-2016-eye-drops] |
| Severe COPD exacerbation | Admission HR 1.91 over a year on metoprolol, 532 people; deaths 11 against 5 | COPD without a cardiac indication | [@dransfield-2019-block-copd] |
| Death and stroke after starting before surgery | Deaths 162 of 5264 against 129 of 5265 in 30 days; stroke RR 1.73 | Non-cardiac surgery | [@bouri-2014-perioperative] |
| Anaphylaxis unresponsive to usual adrenaline doses; thyroid storm on withdrawal; paradoxical hypertension | Label warnings | Prior anaphylaxis; hyperthyroidism; phaeochromocytoma without alpha-blockade | [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label] |
| Mood disorder admission; depression to catatonia | HR 2.11 against ACE inhibitors or ARBs; 299 admissions in 641,685 person-years; exploratory | Treated hypertension | [@boal-2016-mood; @fda-2026-atenolol-label] |
| New-onset type 2 diabetes | RR 1.48 against placebo, 22 trials; glucose-intolerance withdrawals 5.8 against 2.7 per 1000 patient-years on atenolol | Blood-pressure treatment | [@nazarzadeh-2021-diabetes; @mrc-1992-older-adults] |
| Stevens–Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, agranulocytosis, lupus syndrome, mesenteric thrombosis | Listed reactions | Not specified | [@fda-2026-propranolol-label; @fda-2026-atenolol-label] |
| Fatal intentional overdose | 35 deaths among 159 confirmed outcomes | Propranolol for anxiety: 111 of 121 known indications | [@williams-2025-propranolol-poisoning] |

Beta2 blockade removes catecholamine-driven bronchodilation [@fda-2026-propranolol-label; @fda-2026-metoprolol-label]. Renal deterioration on carvedilol in heart failure has been reversed by stopping [@fda-2026-carvedilol-label].

:::industry
In COMMIT (45,852 with acute infarction; industry collaborators), metoprolol caused 11 more cardiogenic shocks per 1000 (5.0% against 3.9%), mostly on days 0 to 1 [@chen-2005-commit-metoprolol].
:::

## Other pathways it affects

Beta receptors are found far beyond the heart [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @uniprot-2025-beta-adrenoceptor-subtypes].

- Receptors (reference database): ADRB1 is annotated in sleep regulation; ADRB2 binds adrenaline about 30-fold more tightly than noradrenaline; ADRB3, mainly in fat, activates lipolysis through Gi and ERK1/2 in cultured cells, and its role in thermogenesis is inferred from the mouse protein [@uniprot-2025-beta-adrenoceptor-subtypes].
- Glucose and fats (people): propranolol impaired recovery from insulin-induced hypoglycaemia in 8 people with type 1 diabetes and blunted glucagon, not in 7 controls [@popp-1982-counterregulation]. Over 16 weeks in 60 hypertensive patients, metoprolol and atenolol lowered insulin-mediated glucose uptake from 5.6 to 4.5 and 4.9 mg/kg/min and raised triglycerides by 0.59 and 0.43 mmol/l [@pollare-1989-insulin].
- Thyroid and eye (people): propranolol may raise T4 and reverse T3 and lower T3 and intraocular pressure [@fda-2026-propranolol-label].
- Urate (people): gout relative risk 1.48 among 29,138 people with hypertension [@choi-2012-gout].
- Brain and energy (people): sleep disorders in 16.7% of 435 infants on propranolol; tiredness 26% against 13% on atenolol [@ema-2026-hemangiol; @fda-2026-atenolol-label].

:::unresolved
In pooled trials, one extra report of fatigue per 57 people treated a year and no significant rise in depressive symptoms were recorded; events were abstracted from reports, not collected systematically [@ko-2002-depression-fatigue].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

Adrenaline and noradrenaline act on a heart-muscle receptor that raises cyclic AMP; beta-blockers occupy it [@uniprot-2025-adrenoceptors; @fda-2026-metoprolol-label].

The mechanism of blood-pressure lowering is not established; lower cardiac output, renin release and central sympathetic outflow are proposed [@fda-2026-bisoprolol-label; @fda-2026-propranolol-label].

## In the body

The liver enzyme CYP2D6 mainly clears metoprolol; half of a bisoprolol dose leaves unchanged in the urine [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2026-bisoprolol-label].

Selectivity is relative: metoprolol increasingly blocks beta2 receptors above 300 nmol/l, bisoprolol from 20 mg [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2026-bisoprolol-label]. Propranolol is non-selective and crosses into brain and placenta; carvedilol also blocks alpha1 receptors; sotalol prolongs the cardiac action potential [@fda-2026-propranolol-label; @fda-2026-carvedilol-label; @fda-2024-sotalol-label]. Adrenaline during long-term propranolol may cause uncontrolled hypertension [@fda-2026-propranolol-label].

## Genetic variation

Responses vary with inherited differences in the receptors and in a liver enzyme [@uniprot-2025-beta-adrenoceptor-subtypes; @fda-2026-metoprolol-label].

*ADRB1* variants are associated with resting heart rate and familial natural short sleep, *ADRB2* Gly-16 with nocturnal asthma, *ADRB3* Arg-64 with weight gain and type 2 diabetes [@uniprot-2025-beta-adrenoceptor-subtypes]. About 8% of Caucasians lack CYP2D6; their metoprolol levels are several-fold higher and cardioselectivity is reduced [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label].

## Benefit against harm

Most hypertension trials were at high risk of bias; three-quarters of beta-blocker users took atenolol [@wiysonge-2017-hypertension].

| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| High blood pressure: 4 placebo-controlled trials, 23,613 people | Stroke 18 against 23 per 1000; deaths 51 against 52 [@wiysonge-2017-hypertension] | New diabetes (above) |
| Aged 65 to 74: atenolol (1102) against placebo (2213), mean 5.8 years | Deaths 26.4 against 24.7 per 1000 patient-years [@mrc-1992-older-adults] | Withdrawals for low pulse 28.0 against 0.0 per 1000 patient-years [@mrc-1992-older-adults] |
| After infarction, ejection fraction 50% or more: 17,801 people, median 3.6 years | Death, infarction or heart failure 8.1% against 8.3% [@kristensen-2026-pooled-beta-blockers] | Deaths 335 against 326 (HR 1.04, 0.89 to 1.21) [@kristensen-2026-pooled-beta-blockers] |

:::industry
In reduced ejection fraction, deaths were 7.3% against 10.8% of 3991 over a mean year in MERIT-HF (one per 28 treated) and 11.8% against 17.3% of 2647 over 1.3 years in CIBIS-II (one per 18) [@merit-hf-1999-metoprolol; @cibis-ii-1999-bisoprolol]. In the US carvedilol trial, 1094 of 1197 entrants were randomised after an open-label run-in in which 5.6% left for adverse events, 0.6% by death [@packer-1996-us-carvedilol].
:::

:::unresolved
Funding statements of MERIT-HF and CIBIS-II cannot be read; both were stopped early, with individual data released only to a manufacturer-supported group; the mainly publicly funded BEST result was not significant [@merit-hf-1999-metoprolol; @cibis-ii-1999-bisoprolol; @best-2001-bucindolol; @cleland-2018-ejection-fraction].
:::

## Without the medicine

In 26,886 adults with coronary disease, myocardial infarction at 6 to 12 months was reduced by exercise-based rehabilitation (RR 0.72, one per 71 treated); a quarter of trials were conducted before contemporary therapy [@dibben-2026-cardiac-rehab]. In 20,995 rural Chinese adults, stroke was lowered from 33.65 to 29.14 per 1000 person-years over 4.74 years by a potassium-enriched salt substitute [@neal-2021-ssass].

## How the recommendations were set

European advice to start beta-blockers before surgery was based on the DECREASE trials; every one investigated by Erasmus Medical Centre was found insecure [@bouri-2014-perioperative].

:::consensus
JNC 8 (2014) did not recommend beta-blockers first, citing one trial with more stroke-driven events than an angiotensin receptor blocker; 4 of 17 panel members had industry ties [@james-2014-jnc8]. ESC (2024) and NICE (NG136) place other classes first [@mcevoy-2024-esc; @nice-2026-hypertension]. NICE advises a beta-blocker in reduced ejection fraction and suggests 12 months after infarction when ejection fraction is not reduced [@nice-2025-heart-failure; @nice-2020-acute-coronary].
:::

## Stopping

Angina or infarction can follow sudden stopping, so tapering is advised over 1 to 2 weeks for metoprolol and about one week for bisoprolol [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2026-bisoprolol-label].

In 12 volunteers, propranolol for 8 days raised lymphocyte beta-receptor density by 43%; density stayed raised for days after the drug left plasma, with larger orthostatic heart-rate rises [@aarons-1980-receptor-density]. After infarction with ejection fraction of 40% or more, interruption was not non-inferior in ABYSS (23.8% against 21.1%, median 3 years) [@silvain-2024-abyss]; pooled with SMART-DECISION, death, infarction or heart-failure admission was 6.5% after stopping against 6.4% [@silvain-2026-pooled-discontinuation].

:::unresolved
A transient relative risk of first coronary heart disease of 4.5 (1.1 to 18.5) was associated with recent stopping in one case-control study [@psaty-1990-withdrawal].
:::

Routine withdrawal before major surgery is advised against [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label; @fda-2026-carvedilol-label]. A prescription is changed together with the prescriber [@fda-2026-metoprolol-label].
