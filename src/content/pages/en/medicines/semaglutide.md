## Serious harms

Semaglutide is prescribed for type 2 diabetes, for weight management and to lower cardiovascular and kidney risk [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acute pancreatitis, sometimes fatal | 0.3 against 0.2 per 100 patient-years on comparators in diabetes trials | Not specified | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label] |
| Gallstones, cholecystitis | Adults 1.6% against 0.7% and 0.6% against 0.2%; 27 extra per 10,000 patient-years in 76 trials | Adolescents; weight-loss trials; higher doses; longer use | [@fda-2026-wegovy-label; @he-2022-gallbladder] |
| Intestinal obstruction, ileus | Not known; for the drug class, 1 extra per 1223 treated for a year against SGLT2 inhibitors, none in semaglutide users | Use beyond 1.6 years | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @ema-2026-wegovy-product-information; @faillie-2022-intestinal-obstruction] |
| Acute kidney injury, some needing haemodialysis | 0.4 against 0.2 per 100 patient-years in weight trials | Vomiting, diarrhoea or dehydration; prior renal impairment; dose escalation | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| Pulmonary aspiration | Rare postmarketing reports | General anaesthesia or deep sedation | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| Diabetic retinopathy complications | 8.2% against 5.2% with prior retinopathy; 0.7% against 0.4% without | Existing retinopathy | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| NAION, ischaemic damage to the optic nerve | Up to 1 in 10,000 users; about 1 extra per 10,000 person-years | Type 2 diabetes | [@ema-2025-naion] |
| Severe hypoglycaemia | 1.5% against 0% with basal insulin over 30 weeks; 0.8% to 1.2% with a sulfonylurea | Insulin or sulfonylurea; prior bariatric surgery | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| Hip or pelvis fracture | Women 24 of 2448 against 5 of 2424; age 75 or more 17 of 703 against 4 of 663 | Women; age 75 or more | [@fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| Thyroid C-cell tumours | In mice and rats; human relevance not determined | Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2: contraindicated | [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| Embryofetal loss and malformation | In rats, rabbits and monkeys at clinical exposures | Pregnancy; stopping at least 2 months before a planned one is advised | [@fda-2026-wegovy-label] |

Gallbladder disease was more frequent than on placebo even after accounting for weight loss [@fda-2026-wegovy-label]. In 20 people, GLP-1 infusion suppressed meal-induced cholecystokinin, the gallbladder-contracting hormone, a mechanism the authors propose [@rehfeld-2018-cholecystokinin]. Across 28 observational studies, GLP-1 receptor agonists were associated with residual gastric contents despite fasting (odds ratio 5.96), not with recorded aspiration (1.04) [@elkin-2025-aspiration]. In independent cohorts, NAION hazard ratios were 2.19 to 2.81 for semaglutide and 1.85 for the class, with onset a median 22.2 months after the first prescription in Denmark [@simonsen-2025-naion; @grauslund-2024-naion; @heberer-2026-naion; @tesfaye-2026-naion]; in a 37-million-person network the incidence rate ratio was 1.32 [@cai-2025-naion]. The US labels of June 2026 do not mention NAION [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label].

:::unresolved
Thyroid cancer was associated with 1 to 3 years of class use in an independent French case-control study (hazard ratio 1.58; medullary 1.78), but not in a Scandinavian cohort against DPP-4 inhibitors, whose semaglutide users had a mean 1.1 years of follow-up; EMA concluded that causality is not supported by the evidence [@bezin-2023-thyroid-cancer; @pasternak-2024-thyroid-cancer; @ema-2023-thyroid-cancer]. In Novo Nordisk-funded pooled trials not powered for NAION, 3 against 5 cases were recorded, all 3 on semaglutide; EMA reports a slightly higher risk in trial data [@vilsboll-2026-naion-trials; @ema-2025-naion].
:::

## Other pathways it affects

The gene for the receptor that semaglutide activates is expressed in the pancreas, heart, kidney, brain and several glands [@karlsson-2021-glp1r].

Its RNA is highest in tuft cells (46.1 nCPM), pancreatic islet cells (23.3), lacrimal and pancreatic acinar cells (19.0, 15.5), other brain neurons (10.8) and proximal tubule cells (5.7); the protein is not detected by antibody staining of tissues [@karlsson-2021-glp1r]. In animals, semaglutide activated neurons in brain regions regulating food intake [@fda-2026-wegovy-label].

| Tissue | Measured in people |
|---|---|
| Stomach | Delayed emptying; after 12 weeks on 1.0 mg, 37% of a solid meal retained at 4 hours against none on placebo, in 20 women with polycystic ovary syndrome [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label; @jensterle-2023-gastric-emptying] |
| Brain | Lower calorie intake [@fda-2026-wegovy-label]; in 48 adults with alcohol use disorder, fewer drinks per drinking day but not fewer drinking days [@hendershot-2025-alcohol] |
| Heart | Resting rate up 1 to 4 beats per minute; rises of 20 or more in 26% against 16% [@fda-2026-wegovy-label] |
| Bone | With liraglutide, hip density 0.013 g/cm² lower than with exercise at similar weight loss, in a secondary analysis [@jensen-2024-bone] |
| Muscle | Median 28.3% of weight lost from muscle-based indices in 35 trials; objective physical function not reported [@batsis-2026-body-composition] |

:::unresolved
With paracetamol absorption, no delay was found at week 20 on 2.4 mg in a company trial, and no clinically relevant effect is stated in the EU label, probably owing to tolerance; the size of a sustained delay is unsettled [@friedrichsen-2021-gastric-emptying; @ema-2026-wegovy-product-information; @jensterle-2023-gastric-emptying].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

Semaglutide is a long-acting form of GLP-1, a hormone that raises insulin and lowers glucagon when blood glucose is high [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @uniprot-2025-hormones].

The glucagon response to induced hypoglycaemia is preserved [@fda-2026-ozempic-label].

## In the body

Half of a dose remains in the body about a week later [@fda-2026-ozempic-label].

Albumin binding slows renal clearance, and a position-8 modification stabilises the peptide against DPP-4 [@fda-2026-ozempic-label]. Slower gastric emptying may alter absorption of oral drugs; oral semaglutide raised levothyroxine exposure by 33% [@fda-2026-wegovy-label].

## Benefit against harm

The heart and kidney indications are limited to people with established cardiovascular disease, or with type 2 diabetes and kidney disease [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2026-wegovy-label].

:::industry
| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| SELECT: 17,604 with cardiovascular disease, BMI 27 or more, no diabetes; mean 3.3 years | Cardiovascular events 6.5% against 8.0%, 1 per 67; cardiovascular death not significantly lower [@lincoff-2023-select] | Stopping for adverse events 16.6% against 8.2%, 1 per 12 [@lincoff-2023-select] |
| FLOW: 3533 with type 2 diabetes and kidney disease; median 3.4 years | Kidney composite 18.7% against 23.2%, 1 per 23 [@perkovic-2024-flow] | Only serious and selected adverse events collected [@fda-2026-ozempic-label] |
| SUSTAIN-6: 3297 with type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular risk; median 2.1 years | Cardiovascular events 6.6% against 8.9%, 1 per 45 [@marso-2016-sustain-6] | Retinopathy complications 3.0% against 1.8%, 1 per 78; blindness 5 against 1 [@marso-2016-sustain-6] |
| STEP 1: 1961 with obesity, no diabetes; 68 weeks | Weight −14.9% against −2.4% [@wilding-2021-step-1] | Serious adverse events 9.8% against 6.4% [@wilding-2021-step-1] |
:::

:::unresolved
Novo Nordisk funded these trials and took part in design wherever its role was read [@marso-2016-sustain-6; @lincoff-2023-select; @perkovic-2024-flow; @wilding-2021-step-1]. In STEP 4, 99 of 902 patients stopped during the run-in, 48 for adverse reactions, before randomisation [@fda-2026-wegovy-label].
:::

## Without the medicine

In an independent randomised trial, 68 of 149 people on a programme based on total diet replacement reached diabetes remission at 12 months, against 6 of 149 controls [@lean-2018-direct].

Thirty years after a 6-year diet and exercise programme for impaired glucose tolerance in Da Qing, cardiovascular events and deaths were fewer (hazard ratio 0.74 each) [@gong-2019-da-qing].

## How the recommendations were set

Approval was extended from type 2 diabetes in 2017 to weight management in 2021 and cardiovascular risk in 2024 [@fda-2026-ozempic-label; @fda-2021-wegovy-approval; @fda-2024-wegovy-cardiovascular].

:::consensus
NICE limits semaglutide to 2 years in specialist services, from a BMI of 35 with a comorbidity or 30 meeting referral criteria; NHS England noted that the equations converting surrogate changes into cardiovascular benefit were unvalidated in people without diabetes [@nice-2023-semaglutide-obesity]. Of the ADA committee's 20 members, 3 disclose ties to Novo Nordisk or Eli Lilly, as do 6 of its 22 subject-matter experts [@ada-2026-obesity].
:::

## Stopping

Lost weight is regained after stopping: 9.9 kg in the first year after semaglutide or tirzepatide, reaching baseline by about 1.5 years in projections from 37 studies, only one longer than a year [@west-2026-weight-regain].

:::industry
In the STEP 1 extension, two thirds of a 17.3% loss was regained within a year among 327 participants, blood pressure was back at baseline, and adverse events were not systematically collected [@wilding-2022-step-1-extension].
:::

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.
