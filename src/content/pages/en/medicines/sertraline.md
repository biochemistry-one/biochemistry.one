summary: Sertraline can raise suicidal thinking under the age of 25 and cause serotonin syndrome, bleeding, dangerously low blood sodium and withdrawal reactions; newborns exposed late in pregnancy can need breathing support, and falls, fractures and seizures are more frequent among older users. The transporter it blocks also clears serotonin in the gut lining and loads it into blood platelets; serotonin receptors are found in the heart, lung arteries, gut and immune cells, and pineal enzymes turn serotonin into melatonin. In trials the average gain over placebo was small, and an estimated 15 in 100 participants benefited substantially beyond placebo.

## Serious harms

Sertraline is prescribed for depression and for panic, social anxiety, obsessive–compulsive, post-traumatic stress and premenstrual dysphoric disorders [@fda-2025-sertraline-label].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suicidal thoughts and behaviour | Short-term antidepressant trials: 14 more per 1000 than placebo under 18, 5 more at 18 to 24 | Under 25 | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |
| Serotonin syndrome, potentially life-threatening | Not stated | Other serotonergic drugs; MAO inhibitors contraindicated | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |
| Bleeding, up to life-threatening | Upper gastrointestinal, 1 year, aged 65 or older: 0.44% against 0.42% untreated | Aspirin, NSAIDs, anticoagulants may add | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings; @coupland-2011-older-people] |
| Intracranial haemorrhage | SSRIs: 6.7 per 100,000 person-years above tricyclics | First 30 days of use | [@renoux-2017-intracranial] |
| Postpartum haemorrhage | Under two-fold increase | Month before delivery | [@mhra-2021-ssri-postpartum-haemorrhage] |
| Hyponatraemia, deaths reported | 1 year, aged 65 or older: 0.29% on sertraline and untreated, 0.44% on SSRIs | Older age, diuretics, volume depletion | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings; @coupland-2011-older-people] |
| Falls, fractures, seizures | 1 year: 5.57%, 2.80%, 0.55%, against 3.46%, 1.76%, 0.21% untreated (observational) | Aged 65 or older | [@coupland-2011-falls-mortality] |
| Torsade de pointes | Postmarketing reports | Pimozide, contraindicated; QT-prolonging drugs | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |
| Mania or hypomania | 0.4% in trials | Bipolar disorder | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |
| Angle-closure glaucoma | Not stated | Untreated narrow angles | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |
| Liver failure, pancreatitis, agranulocytosis, aplastic anaemia, thrombocytopenia, Stevens–Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, reversible cerebral vasoconstriction, pulmonary hypertension, rhabdomyolysis, maculopathy, sexual dysfunction lasting after stopping | Postmarketing reports | Not stated | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings; @ema-2019-persistent-sexual-dysfunction] |
| Newborn toxicity or withdrawal, needing respiratory support | Postmarketing reports | Late third-trimester exposure | [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |
| Newborn pulmonary hypertension | 2.9 to 3.5 extra per 1000 infants | Late-pregnancy exposure | [@grigoriadis-2014-pphn] |
| Severe withdrawal symptoms | 2.8% against 0.6% after placebo | Stopping, particularly abruptly | [@henssler-2024-discontinuation; @fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] |

In regulators' clinical study reports, 27 suicide attempts and 32 ideation events had been coded as emotional lability or worsening depression; in 16 sertraline trials, a terminology unable to code akathisia or suicidal ideation was used [@sharma-2016-suicidality].

MAO inhibitors raise serotonin syndrome risk by impairing serotonin breakdown [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings]. Sertraline blocks serotonin uptake into platelets, and impaired platelet aggregation is the proposed cause of bleeding [@fda-2025-sertraline-label; @mhra-2021-ssri-postpartum-haemorrhage]. Hyponatraemia is often attributed to inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings].

In older people, most risks were highest in the first 28 days after starting and after stopping; confounding by indication is possible [@coupland-2011-falls-mortality]. In a randomised trial of fluoxetine, another SSRI, fractures were recorded in 45 of 1564 against 23 of 1563 stroke patients over 6 months, about 1 extra per 71 treated [@focus-2019-fluoxetine]. Among women with depression, the adjusted odds ratio for newborn pulmonary hypertension after late-pregnancy SSRI use was 1.10 (95% CI 0.94 to 1.29) [@huybrechts-2015-pphn]. Transient, asymptomatic aminotransferase rises and rare clinically apparent liver injury are linked to sertraline [@livertox-2020-sertraline].

## Other pathways it affects

The transporter that sertraline blocks also moves serotonin in the gut lining and into blood platelets [@uniprot-2025-peripheral-serotonin; @uniprot-2025-serotonin].

| Tissue | Serotonin protein | Measured in people |
|---|---|---|
| Gut lining | SERT; 5-HT3 and 5-HT4 receptors [@uniprot-2025-peripheral-serotonin] | Nausea 26% against 12% on placebo, diarrhoea 20% against 10% [@fda-2025-sertraline-label] |
| Platelets | SERT fills dense granules; 5-HT2A [@uniprot-2025-serotonin; @uniprot-2025-peripheral-serotonin] | Bleeding, above |
| Bone, heart, pulmonary artery, liver, kidney | 5-HT2B; roles inferred from animals [@uniprot-2025-peripheral-serotonin] | Fractures and pulmonary hypertension, above |

Shown in mice: 6 weeks of fluoxetine caused bone loss through raised sympathetic output, prevented by propranolol [@ortuno-2016-bone].

Measured in people: new weight gain of 5% or more was recorded at 11.2 against 8.1 per 100 person-years with and without antidepressants, 1 extra per 59 patient-years [@gafoor-2018-weight]. Ejaculation failure was reported in 8% of men against 1% on placebo, rates described in the label as possibly underestimated [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings].

:::unresolved
5-HT1A is found in lymph nodes, thymus, spleen and activated T cells, and pineal AANAT and ASMT convert serotonin to melatonin; sertraline's effects there were unmeasured in the studies cited here [@uniprot-2025-peripheral-serotonin].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

Sertraline blocks the transporter that returns serotonin, a signalling molecule, into the nerve cell that released it [@uniprot-2025-serotonin; @fda-2025-sertraline-label].

SERT (SLC6A4) moves serotonin back with Na+ in exchange for K+, and MAO-A then deaminates it [@uniprot-2025-serotonin].

::pathway{id="serotonin-synapse"}

:::unresolved
In an umbrella review, depression was not consistently associated with serotonin concentrations or transporter genotype [@moncrieff-2023-serotonin]. Critics cite tryptophan depletion [@jauhar-2023-serotonin-response], and reduced serotonin release was inferred from PET in 17 patients [@erritzoe-2023-serotonin-release].
:::

## In the body

Half of the sertraline in blood is cleared in about a day [@fda-2025-sertraline-label].

CYP2C19 and CYP2B6 are regarded as the most relevant metabolising enzymes [@braten-2020-cyp2c19; @braten-2022-cyp2b6]. Exposure was about threefold higher in 10 patients with mild hepatic impairment [@fda-2025-sertraline-label]. Sertraline inhibits CYP2D6 and may raise exposure to flecainide, propafenone and metoprolol [@fda-2025-sertraline-label].

## Genetic variation

Inherited enzyme variants alter the amount of sertraline in the blood [@braten-2020-cyp2c19].

Serum sertraline was 2.68-fold higher in CYP2C19 poor metabolisers among 1202 patients [@braten-2020-cyp2c19].

:::consensus
CPIC suggests a 50% lower maintenance dose or another drug for CYP2C19 poor metabolisers [@bousman-2023-cpic-ssri].
:::

## Benefit against harm

On average, people improved slightly more on antidepressants than on placebo [@stone-2022-fda-response].

| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| Depression, acute placebo-controlled trials | Large response 24.5% against 9.6% in 232 trials, 1 per 6.7 treated [@stone-2022-fda-response] | Serious adverse events 2.7% against 2.1% in 44 SSRI trials, 1 per 138 [@jakobsen-2017-ssri] |
| Aged 65 or older | Suicidal thoughts or behaviour, 6 fewer per 1000 [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings] | Death in 1 year, 10.20% against 7.04% untreated, observational [@coupland-2011-falls-mortality] |

The mean difference was 1.75 Hamilton points, 1.1 at a baseline of 16 and 2.5 at 29.6 [@stone-2022-fda-response]. Drug companies funded 409 of 522 trials in a network meta-analysis of 21 antidepressants [@cipriani-2018-antidepressants]. In the NIHR-funded PANDA trial of 655 primary-care adults, depression scores on sertraline were not significantly lower at 6 weeks, 13% lower at 12 weeks, and anxiety scores 21% lower at 6 weeks [@lewis-2019-panda].

:::unresolved
All 131 SSRI trials in one review were at high risk of bias, and long-term harm data were almost absent [@jakobsen-2017-ssri].
:::

## Without the medicine

Exercise and talking therapy were compared with antidepressants in independent randomised trials [@blumenthal-2007-smile; @amick-2015-cbt].

In SMILE, 16-week remission was 45% with supervised exercise, 40% at home, 47% with sertraline and 31% with placebo (p=0.057), among 202 adults [@blumenthal-2007-smile]. In five trials of 660 patients, response was 44.2% with second-generation antidepressants and 45.5% with cognitive behavioural therapy [@amick-2015-cbt].

## How the recommendations were set

Selective publication of SSRI trials was documented in regulators' files [@melander-2003-selective-reporting].

Mean scores were missing from FDA reviews for four non-significant sertraline trials, with 486 patients, 38% of those in sertraline trials [@kirsch-2008-severity].

:::consensus
In 2010 the APA recommended an antidepressant or psychotherapy as initial treatment for mild to moderate depression; all six Work Group members disclosed drug-company ties [@apa-2010-depression]. In 2022 NICE ranked SSRIs ninth of eleven options for less severe depression (PHQ-9 below 16) [@nice-2022-depression-options].
:::

## Stopping

Dizziness, electric-shock sensations, anxiety and seizures have been reported after stopping, particularly abruptly; gradual reduction is recommended [@fda-2025-sertraline-warnings].

Across 79 studies, discontinuation symptoms were recorded in 31% after stopping an antidepressant and 17% after placebo, 8 percentage points more in randomised trials; in 5 sertraline studies, 18% [@henssler-2024-discontinuation]. In ANTLER, 56% of 240 people tapered to placebo over 1 to 2 months relapsed within 52 weeks, against 39% of 238 who continued, and withdrawal scores were higher [@lewis-2021-antler]. In a Cochrane review of 33 trials, most tapers were 4 weeks or shorter and withdrawal was generally not assessed [@van-leeuwen-2021-discontinuation].

:::unresolved
In 74% of the cohorts in that review, funding was definitely or probably pharmaceutical; in the 5 studies measuring withdrawal systematically, pooled incidence was 55% [@moncrieff-2025-withdrawal-reanalysis]. Early relapse after rapid switches to placebo is possibly attributable to withdrawal reactions [@hengartner-2021-relapse-withdrawal].
:::

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.
