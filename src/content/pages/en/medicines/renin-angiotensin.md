summary: These medicines can cause swelling of the lips, tongue and throat that can block breathing, a dangerous rise in blood potassium, sudden kidney failure, and injury or death of an unborn child exposed in later pregnancy. The enzyme that ACE inhibitors block also breaks down bradykinin and other signalling molecules in many tissues, which is presumed to cause a persistent dry cough, and blocking the hormone chain raises renin and alters potassium handling and the risk of gout. In trials in high blood pressure, ACE inhibitors lowered deaths over about five years from 13.6% to 11.3%; in mild high blood pressure without heart disease, the effect on deaths was uncertain.

## Serious harms

ACE inhibitors such as lisinopril and angiotensin receptor blockers such as losartan are prescribed for high blood pressure, heart failure, after myocardial infarction and for diabetic kidney disease [@fda-2025-lisinopril; @fda-2026-losartan].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Angio-oedema, sometimes fatal | 4.38 per 1000 person-years in 1,845,138 ACE-inhibitor starters, 1.67 on beta-blockers; airway obstruction 0.43 | Women, Black patients, mTOR or neprilysin inhibitors | [@toh-2012-angioedema; @fda-2026-enalapril] |
| Hyperkalaemia | Above 5.7 mEq/l in 1% to 2.2% in hypertension | Kidney impairment, diabetes, potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements or salt substitutes | [@fda-2025-lisinopril; @fda-2026-ramipril] |
| Sudden death with co-trimoxazole | About 3 per 1000 prescriptions in 14 days | Aged 66 or over | [@fralick-2014-co-trimoxazole] |
| Acute kidney injury | Admissions 7 per 10,000 person-years in 487,372 antihypertensive users; rate ratio 1.31 with a diuretic plus an NSAID | Renal artery stenosis, heart failure, volume depletion | [@lapi-2013-triple-whammy; @fda-2026-enalapril] |
| Fetal kidney failure and death | Boxed warning; not quantified | Second and third trimesters | [@fda-2025-lisinopril] |
| Excessive hypotension | Persistent in 9.0% against 3.7% within 6 weeks of infarction | Heart failure, salt or volume depletion, dialysis | [@fda-2025-lisinopril; @fda-2026-enalapril] |
| Neutropenia, agranulocytosis | 1 in over 8600 to 3.7%, by kidney function and collagen vascular disease; about 13% of cases fatal | Captopril users | [@fda-2024-captopril] |
| Nephrotic syndrome | One-fifth of the 0.7% with proteinuria above 1 g/day | Captopril users | [@fda-2024-captopril] |
| Hepatic necrosis; anaphylactoid and severe skin reactions; pancreatitis | Rare or post-marketing reports | Venom desensitisation, high-flux dialysis, LDL apheresis | [@fda-2026-enalapril] |
| Sprue-like enteropathy; cardiovascular death | Not quantified; 15 against 3 on placebo in 4447, not conclusive | Olmesartan, after months to years; 40 mg in type 2 diabetes | [@fda-2025-olmesartan] |
| Lung cancer | 1.6 against 1.2 per 1000 person-years on receptor blockers; 992,061 people, mean 6.4 years | ACE inhibitors beyond 10 years | [@hicks-2018-lung-cancer] |
| Cancer from nitrosamine impurities | Up to 22 extra lifetime cancers per 100,000 after 6 years, estimated; observed liver cancer 3.7 extra per 100,000 person-years | Contaminated sartans, found in 2018 | [@ema-2019-sartan-nitrosamines; @mansouri-2022-valsartan] |

Aldosterone, which these drugs lower, drives potassium excretion [@fda-2026-losartan; @turck-2019-sodium]. Angio-oedema is most frequent in the first 30 days (9.68 per 1000 person-years) but was still 2.59 at 9 to 12 months [@toh-2012-angioedema]. In PARADIGM-HF it occurred in 0.5% on sacubitril-valsartan against 0.2% on enalapril, and in 2.4% against 0.5% of Black patients; its run-ins first removed 10.5% and 10.4% of patients, so reported adverse reaction rates are lower than expected in practice [@fda-2026-sacubitril-valsartan]. Oligohydramnios may appear only after irreversible fetal injury [@fda-2026-enalapril]. Kidney changes and neutropenia usually reverse on stopping [@fda-2026-enalapril; @fda-2024-captopril].

:::industry
In ALLHAT, with Pfizer support, angio-oedema affected 38 of 9054 on lisinopril against 8 of 15,255 on chlorthalidone over a mean 4.9 years, with one death [@allhat-2002-lisinopril].
:::

:::unresolved
That bradykinin mediates angio-oedema rests on plasma levels in one patient; icatibant, a bradykinin-receptor blocker, outperformed corticosteroid plus antihistamine in 27 patients but not placebo in 121 [@nussberger-1998-bradykinin; @bas-2015-icatibant; @sinert-2017-icatibant]. First-trimester malformation risk rose in one cohort but not against hypertensive mothers in two larger ones; the EMA cannot exclude a small risk for either class [@cooper-2006-malformations; @li-2011-first-trimester; @bateman-2017-malformations; @ema-2026-irbesartan].
:::

## Other pathways it affects

ACE, found in most tissues, breaks down several signalling peptides [@uniprot-2025-raas].

- Peptides (reference database): bradykinin, substance P, neurotensin, enkephalins, cholecystokinin and amyloid-beta; a testis isoform is required for male fertility [@uniprot-2025-raas].
- Airways (people): cough, presumed from bradykinin, affected almost 12% over a year on ramipril, resolving on stopping; in head-to-head trials it caused 22 of 51 stated withdrawals, against 2 of 49 on receptor blockers [@fda-2026-ramipril; @li-2014-acei-arb].
- Kidney (people): angiotensin II constricts the efferent arteriole; creatinine rose 30% or more in 1.7% of 122,363 starters, associated with death (rate ratio 1.84) [@schmidt-2017-creatinine].
- Renin (labels): renin activity doubles to triples on losartan [@fda-2026-losartan].
- Urate (people): gout relative risk 0.81 on losartan, 1.24 on ACE inhibitors [@choi-2012-gout].
- Glucose (trials, labels): new type 2 diabetes relative risk 0.84 against placebo for each class; irbesartan may induce hypoglycaemia [@nazarzadeh-2021-diabetes; @ema-2026-irbesartan].

:::unresolved
The enalapril label lists cough in 1.3%; 125 trials gave a pooled 11.48% [@fda-2026-enalapril; @bangalore-2010-cough].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

These medicines lower blood pressure by blocking a hormone chain that constricts arteries and retains salt [@fda-2025-lisinopril; @fda-2026-losartan; @turck-2019-sodium].

::pathway{id="renin-angiotensin"}

ACE inhibitors lower angiotensin II formation; losartan binds the AT1 receptor with about 1000-fold greater affinity than AT2 [@fda-2025-lisinopril; @fda-2026-losartan]. ACE2, not inhibited by ACE inhibitors, converts angiotensin II to angiotensin-(1-7) [@uniprot-2025-raas].

## In the body

Combining two drugs that block this chain adds harm without lowering deaths [@makani-2013-dual-blockade].

Across 33 trials (68,405 patients, mean 52 weeks), mortality was unchanged (relative risk 0.97) and heart-failure admissions fell by 18%, while hyperkalaemia rose from 4.9% to 9.6% and renal failure by 41% [@makani-2013-dual-blockade].

## Genetic variation

Inherited defects in any of four components of this chain disrupt kidney development before birth [@uniprot-2025-raas].

Variants in *REN*, *AGT*, *ACE* or *AGTR1* cause renal tubular dysgenesis [@uniprot-2025-raas].

## Benefit against harm

In three placebo-controlled trials in hypertension (6002 patients), ACE inhibitors lowered deaths over 4.9 years from 13.6% to 11.3% (relative risk 0.83), one per 43 treated [@wright-2018-first-line].

No such trial of a first-line receptor blocker was found [@wright-2018-first-line].

| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| Mild hypertension, no cardiovascular disease, any drug class | Deaths: relative risk 0.85 (0.64 to 1.14; 9124 people); stroke 0.41; low certainty [@wang-2026-mild-hypertension] | Withdrawal for adverse effects: relative risk 4.80, from one trial's whole population [@wang-2026-mild-hypertension] |
| Symptomatic heart failure, ejection fraction 35% or less | Deaths 11% lower over up to 55 months (2569); class IV one-year survival 64% against 48% (253) [@fda-2026-enalapril] | Hypotension 6.7%, syncope 2.2%, hyperkalaemia 3.8% [@fda-2026-enalapril] |
| Type 2 diabetic nephropathy, irbesartan, 2.6 years | Kidney composite about 39% to 33%, not evident in women or Black patients; deaths unchanged [@ema-2026-irbesartan] | Potassium 5.5 mEq/l or more: 46.3% against 26.3% [@ema-2026-irbesartan] |

:::unresolved
Trandolapril did not reduce events in 8290 lower-risk coronary patients over 4.8 years (21.9% against 22.5%) [@braunwald-2004-peace]. Withdrawals for adverse effects could not be calculated for first-line ACE inhibitors [@wright-2018-first-line].
:::

## Without the medicine

In a randomised trial, replacing part of the salt with potassium salt lowered stroke and death [@neal-2021-ssass].

Among 20,995 Chinese villagers, 72.6% with previous stroke, stroke fell from 33.65 to 29.14 and death from 44.61 to 39.28 per 1000 person-years over 4.74 years [@neal-2021-ssass]. The trial excluded households using potassium-sparing diuretics or supplements and did not measure serum potassium, though 22.8% took these drugs [@neal-2021-ssass].

## How the recommendations were set

Lowering the US threshold from 140/90 to 130/80 mm Hg in 2017 raised the estimated share of adults with high blood pressure from 31.9% to 45.6% [@muntner-2018-acc-aha-impact].

The guideline's own writing committee produced the estimate [@muntner-2018-acc-aha-impact].

:::consensus
NICE offers a calcium channel blocker first from age 55 and for people of Black African or African-Caribbean origin without type 2 diabetes, adding these drugs at step 2 or 3 [@nice-2026-hypertension].
:::

:::unresolved
At least 80% of authors of four ESC guidelines, heart failure among them, had financial conflicts [@hinton-2021-esc-conflicts].
:::

## Stopping

Stopping these drugs 48 hours before major surgery did not change death or major complications [@legrand-2024-stop-or-not].

In 2222 patients, rates were 22% in each group at 28 days; intraoperative hypotension was 54% when continued against 41% [@legrand-2024-stop-or-not]. With eGFR below 30 ml/min/1.73 m², stopping did not preserve kidney function over 3 years (411 patients) [@bhandari-2022-stop-acei]. In recovered dilated cardiomyopathy, phased withdrawal of heart-failure drugs led to relapse in 11 of 25 within 6 months, against none of 26 [@halliday-2019-tred-hf].

:::unresolved
Labels state that abrupt withdrawal has not been associated with a rapid rise in pressure; withdrawal trials did not report falls [@fda-2025-lisinopril; @gnjidic-2025-antihypertensive-withdrawal].
:::

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.
