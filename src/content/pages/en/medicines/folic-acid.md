## Serious harms

Folic acid is prescribed for folate-deficiency anaemia, to prevent a recurrent neural tube defect in pregnancy and to reduce methotrexate side effects [@fda-2026-folic-acid-label; @mhra-2025-folic-acid-spc; @shea-2013-methotrexate].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| B12-deficiency nerve and spinal cord damage, progressing while anaemia is corrected | 32 of 70 with pernicious anaemia on 5 mg daily relapsed neurologically over 3.5 years | Untreated B12 deficiency, including lifelong vegetarians | [@mhra-2025-folic-acid-spc; @turck-2023-folate] |
| Anaphylaxis | 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 1000 | Not stated | [@mhra-2025-folic-acid-spc; @ema-2018-folic-acid-psusa] |
| Loss of seizure control | Not quantified | Phenytoin, phenobarbital or primidone | [@fda-2026-folic-acid-label; @mhra-2025-folic-acid-spc] |
| Faster loss of kidney function; vascular events or death | GFR fell 5.8 mL/min/1.73 m² more; events 23.5% against 14.4% of 238 at 36 months (HR 2.0), 1 per 11 | Diabetic nephropathy; with B6 and B12 | [@house-2010-divine] |
| Prostate cancer | 10-year probability 9.7% against 3.3% of 643 men (HR 2.63), 1 per 16 | 1 mg daily after colorectal adenoma; secondary analysis | [@figueiredo-2009-prostate] |

Folic acid enters the cell's folate pool through dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR), independently of vitamin B12 [@turck-2023-folate; @gherasim-2013-cobalamin]. In B12 deficiency, it restarts DNA synthesis in bone marrow but cannot restore the methionine cycle [@turck-2023-folate]. The neuropathy, which can occur without anaemia, is attributed to lost methionine synthase activity [@turck-2023-folate; @gherasim-2013-cobalamin].

::pathway{id="one-carbon"}

Neurological relapses typically occurred within 2 years; signs often resolved with cobalamin but became irreversible in several cases [@turck-2023-folate]. Under the US label, doses above 0.1 mg daily may obscure pernicious anaemia [@fda-2026-folic-acid-label]. EFSA rated the certainty that folic acid itself worsens the neuropathy as low (15 to 50% probability) [@turck-2023-folate]. B12 deficiency was associated with 2 years or more of proton pump inhibitor use (OR 1.65; 25,956 cases, 184,199 controls) and may affect up to 1 in 10 metformin users [@lam-2013-b12; @mhra-2022-metformin-b12].

Across 13 trials, first cancers occurred in 7.7% of 24,799 given folic acid and 7.3% of 24,822 controls over a mean 5.5 years (RR 1.06, 0.99 to 1.13); only cancers during treatment were counted [@vollset-2013-cancer].

:::industry
In Norwegian trials supported by Alpharma, 0.8 mg with B12 for a median 39 months in 6837 patients with ischaemic heart disease was followed over a median 78 months by cancer in 10.0% against 8.4% (HR 1.21) and all-cause death in 16.1% against 13.8% (HR 1.18), 1 per 44 [@ebbing-2009-cancer]. Under the UK label, folate should not be used routinely with coronary stents [@mhra-2025-folic-acid-spc]. In a trial part-funded by Medice, 1.2 mg with B6 and B12 after stenting was followed by repeat revascularisation in 50 of 316 (15.8%) against 34 of 320 (10.6%) by 250 days, 1 per 19 [@lange-2004-restenosis].
:::

:::unresolved
In observational registers, children of mothers with epilepsy exposed in pregnancy to a mean 4.3 mg folic acid had cancer by age 20 in 1.4% against 0.6% (5934 exposed; HR 2.7, 1.2 to 6.3); without maternal epilepsy, HR was 1.1 (0.9 to 1.4) [@vegrim-2022-childhood-cancer].
:::

## Other pathways it affects

Under US fortification, unmetabolised folic acid was detectable in the blood of over 95% of people, with or without supplements [@pfeiffer-2015-umfa].

The reduced folate carrier, the main route into tissues including brain, has about 100 times lower affinity for folic acid than for reduced folates [@turck-2023-folate].

In people, cord-blood methylation at LINE-1, IGF2 and BDNF was lower when 400 µg was continued in later pregnancy (86 samples), without a shown health effect [@caffrey-2018-methylation; @turck-2023-folate]. In one rat study, folic acid at one dietary level increased colon aberrant crypt foci when started before a carcinogen and reduced them when started after [@turck-2023-folate].

:::unresolved
Natural killer cell cytotoxicity was about 23% lower with detectable unmetabolised folic acid in 105 women, and fell on 5 mg daily in 30 adults without controls [@troen-2006-nk-cells; @paniz-2017-nk-cells].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

Folate is needed to make DNA; dietary deficiency causes anaemia with large red cells [@ducker-2017-one-carbon].

DHFR reduces folic acid to tetrahydrofolate, which binds the one-carbon units used in DNA synthesis [@turck-2023-folate; @ducker-2017-one-carbon].

## In the body

Human gut and liver convert folic acid slowly; above 1 mg daily, most of the excess is excreted unchanged in urine [@patanwala-2014-gut; @fda-2026-folic-acid-label].

In 5 adults, 80 ± 12% of labelled portal-vein folate was unchanged folic acid 15 minutes after 220 µg [@patanwala-2014-gut]. DHFR activity per gram of 6 human livers was under 2% of rat values [@bailey-2009-dhfr]. Fluorouracil toxicity may occur with folic acid; trimethoprim or sulphonamides may reduce its effect, potentially seriously in megaloblastic anaemia [@mhra-2025-folic-acid-spc]. Methotrexate and trimethoprim inhibit DHFR; fluorouracil inhibits thymidylate synthase [@ducker-2017-one-carbon].

## Genetic variation

A common gene variant is associated with lower blood folate; it alters MTHFR, an enzyme other than DHFR [@tsang-2015-mthfr-folate; @ducker-2017-one-carbon].

About 10 to 15% of people of European or Japanese origin are homozygous for 677C>T (TT) [@yamada-2001-mthfr]. TT women had 16% lower red cell folate than CC women [@tsang-2015-mthfr-folate].

:::consensus
The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics finds no evidence that specific treatments reduce genotype-related risks [@hickey-2013-acmg-mthfr].
:::

## Benefit against harm

Folic acid before conception lowers the risk of neural tube defects, malformations of brain and spinal cord [@de-regil-2015-cochrane].

After a previous affected pregnancy, with 4 mg daily to 12 weeks, defects occurred in 6 of 593 (1.0%) against 21 of 602 (3.5%) pregnancies (RR 0.28), 1 prevented per 40 women [@mrc-1991-vitamin-study]. Across 5 trials (6708 births), RR was 0.31 (0.17 to 0.58); only one trial was at low risk of bias, and a single trial of 261 tested 0.4 mg or less [@de-regil-2015-cochrane].

| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| Methotrexate for rheumatoid arthritis; 624 adults, 8 to 52 weeks | Transaminase rise 5% against 21% (RR 0.23; NNT 6) | No significant loss of methotrexate effect [@shea-2013-methotrexate] |
| Homocysteine-lowering B vitamins; 15 trials, 71,422 participants, 1 to 7.3 years | Stroke 4.3% against 5.1% (RR 0.90); myocardial infarction and death unchanged | Serious adverse events 8.3% against 8.5% (RR 1.07, 1.00 to 1.14) [@marti-carvajal-2017-homocysteine] |

:::unresolved
In a non-randomised Chinese campaign of 400 µg, calculated pregnancies per defect avoided were about 263 in the high-risk north and 2500 in the south [@berry-1999-china].
:::

## Without the medicine

Folate occurs in dark green vegetables, legumes, nuts, liver and yeast, with considerable cooking losses [@turck-2023-folate].

:::unresolved
In four food-folate trials, three with industry co-funding, blood folate and homocysteine were measured; neural tube defects were not [@winkels-2007-food-folate; @brouwer-1999-dietary-folate; @ashfield-watt-2002-diet; @cuskelly-1996-food-folate].
:::

## How the recommendations were set

The European upper level of 1000 µg a day, retained in 2023, is based on the risk that neurological symptoms progress in B12 deficiency [@turck-2023-folate].

:::consensus
The Institute of Medicine (1998) took 5 mg as the lowest-observed-adverse-effect level and divided it by 5; its funders included a corporate fund with Roche Vitamins among donors [@iom-1998-folate]. The US Preventive Services Task Force finds adequate evidence that usual doses are not associated with serious harms [@uspstf-2023-folic-acid].
:::

:::unresolved
Wald and colleagues note that the IOM drew on 23 studies, mostly from the 1950s, and attribute the neuropathy to untreated B12 deficiency [@wald-2018-upper-level]. The periconceptional studies were not designed to assess adverse effects, and side effects were reported in only one trial of the Cochrane review [@iom-1998-folate; @de-regil-2015-cochrane].
:::

## Stopping

Blood folate falls when folic acid is stopped in pregnancy [@mcnulty-2013-fasstt].

In 60 women randomised to stop 400 µg at 14 weeks, serum folate fell from 45.7 to 19.5 nmol/L by week 36 and homocysteine rose from 6.6 to 7.6 µmol/L [@mcnulty-2013-fasstt].

:::unresolved
In the adenoma trial, risk ratios were attenuated and null in the 7.5 years after treatment [@turck-2023-folate].
:::

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.
