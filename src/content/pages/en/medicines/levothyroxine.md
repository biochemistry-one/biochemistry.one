summary: Too much levothyroxine can cause atrial fibrillation, angina, heart failure and heart attack, and doses that push the pituitary hormone TSH very low are linked with more strokes, fractures, memory disorders and deaths; started before steroid replacement in adrenal failure it can trigger an adrenal crisis, and in children overtreatment can close skull sutures or growth plates early. Thyroid hormone also acts on bone turnover, cholesterol handling in the liver, energy use in muscle, the brain and blood sugar control. In trials in people with a mildly raised TSH, it brought the blood test into range but did not improve symptoms or quality of life, and in older adults it did not significantly change heart events or deaths.

## Serious harms

Levothyroxine is prescribed when the thyroid gland makes too little hormone, and in thyroid cancer to suppress thyrotropin (TSH), the pituitary hormone that stimulates the gland [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-label; @brent-2012-thyroid].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atrial fibrillation and other dysrhythmias | 367 events in 17,684 users over a median 4.5 years; hazard ratio (HR) 1.6 at TSH 0.03 mU/l or less | Older people | [@flynn-2010-tsh-outcomes; @abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Cardiovascular events and death | 2144 events in the same users, HR 1.37 at that TSH; 75,963 cardiovascular deaths in 705,307 veterans over a median 4 years, HR 1.39 at TSH below 0.1 mIU/l | Also undertreatment: HR 2.67 at TSH above 20 | [@flynn-2010-tsh-outcomes; @evron-2022-cardiovascular-mortality] |
| Stroke | 41,931 of 663,809 veterans over a median 59 months; odds ratio 1.33 at TSH below 0.1 mIU/l | Also TSH above 5.5 | [@papaleontiou-2021-stroke] |
| Angina, heart failure, myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest | Not quantified | Elderly people; cardiovascular disease | [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Osteoporotic fracture | 562 in the same users, HR 2.02 at that TSH; 10.4% of 213,511 users aged 70 or more over a mean 3.8 years | Higher doses; women after the menopause | [@flynn-2010-tsh-outcomes; @turner-2011-fractures; @abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Cognitive impairment or dementia | By age 75, 11.0% after low TSH against 6.4% without, of 65,931 | Aged 65 or more; HR 1.34 when treatment lowered TSH | [@adams-2023-cognitive-disorders] |
| Death | HR 1.18 at TSH below 0.1 and 2.21 above 10 mIU/l, against 2 to 2.5, in 160,439 over a median 6 years | Over- and undertreatment | [@thayakaran-2019-tsh] |
| Acute adrenal crisis | Not quantified | Uncorrected adrenal insufficiency | [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Cerebral embolism, shock, coma, death | Reported in overdose | Overdose, including use for weight loss | [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Craniosynostosis, early growth-plate closure, pseudotumour cerebri, slipped capital femoral epiphysis | Not quantified | Infants and children | [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Seizures; angio-oedema and serum sickness | Rare; not quantified | Start of therapy; sensitivity to inactive ingredients | [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |

Most adverse reactions are those of excess hormone, which may raise heart rate, cardiac wall thickness and contractility [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed]. Excess is common: 41.0% of 339 users aged 65 or more had TSH below 0.45 mU/l [@somwaru-2009-over-replacement]. In people, hyperthyroidism shifts bone remodelling towards resorption, with a net loss of about 10% per cycle [@bassett-2016-skeleton]. A dose change may take 4 to 6 weeks to act fully [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-label].

:::unresolved
Independent cohorts disagree: in UK THIN data, TSH below 0.1 mIU/l was associated with death but not with atrial fibrillation or fractures, and the authors note possible reverse causality [@thayakaran-2019-tsh]. Among 8,573,313 Swedes, levothyroxine use was associated with cancer (HR 1.08 in women, 1.06 in men); funding could not be read [@wandell-2020-cancer].
:::

## Other pathways it affects

Thyroid hormone acts through two receptors, TRα and TRβ, whose amounts differ between tissues [@mullur-2014-metabolism].

| Tissue | Mechanism | Known in people |
|---|---|---|
| Bone | In mice, T3 is anabolic in the growing and catabolic in the adult skeleton, mainly through TRα1 [@bassett-2016-skeleton] | Over-replacement may raise resorption and lower density [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |
| Liver | T3 stimulates transcription of the LDL receptor and HMG-CoA reductase; many such mechanisms come from rodents [@mullur-2014-metabolism] | No significant change in LDL cholesterol at 12 months in 286 older adults, post hoc [@ao-2026-cardiometabolic] |
| Muscle and fat | T3 stimulates SERCA, which hydrolyses ATP [@mullur-2014-metabolism] | Resting energy expenditure tracked thyroid status in 138 treated adults; weight did not differ [@samuels-2018-dose-energy] |
| Brain | DIO2 activates T4 in the hypothalamus [@mullur-2014-metabolism] | Doses reaching mean TSH of 1.85 to 9.49 mU/l left mood and memory unchanged [@samuels-2018-dose-mood] |
| Glucose and cortisol | Increases glucocorticoid clearance [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] | May worsen glycaemic control in diabetes [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed] |

:::unresolved
Despite normal TSH, 15.2% of 1811 athyreotic patients on levothyroxine had free T3 below the control range [@gullo-2011-athyreotic].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

Levothyroxine is synthetic thyroxine (T4), the main hormone the thyroid releases [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-label; @gereben-2008-deiodinases]. In subclinical hypothyroidism TSH is raised and free T4 normal [@stott-2017-trust].

Thyroid peroxidase iodinates thyroglobulin and couples its iodotyrosines into T4; DIO1 and DIO2 convert T4 to active T3, and DIO3 inactivates both [@milacic-2024-reactome; @gereben-2008-deiodinases]. Pituitary DIO2 forms the T3 through which T4 suppresses TSH, so TSH guides the dose in primary hypothyroidism [@gereben-2008-deiodinases; @abbvie-2024-synthroid-label].

## In the body

Levothyroxine is absorbed best from an empty stomach and is cleared slowly, with a half-life of 6 to 7 days [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-label].

Proton pump inhibitors, antacids, calcium carbonate, ferrous sulfate, bile acid sequestrants and dietary fibre may reduce absorption [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed]. With tricyclic antidepressants levothyroxine may increase arrhythmia risk, and with sympathomimetics the risk of coronary insufficiency in coronary artery disease [@abbvie-2024-synthroid-dailymed]. The EMA judged biotin interference with thyroid tests causal, with significant potential for mismanagement [@ema-2022-levothyroxine-psusa].

:::consensus
The American Thyroid Association calls the 4-hour separation from calcium and iron traditional but untested [@jonklaas-2014-ata].
:::

## Genetic variation

Rare mutations show which tissues depend on each thyroid hormone receptor [@bochukova-2012-thra].

A child with a dominant-negative *THRA* mutation had growth retardation, constipation and bradycardia despite near-normal thyroid tests; thyroxine suppressed TSH but improved growth, heart rate and gut function little [@bochukova-2012-thra]. *DIO2* Thr92Ala was associated with lower enzyme velocity in human thyroid and muscle [@gereben-2008-deiodinases].

## Benefit against harm

In 21 trials with 2192 adults with mildly raised TSH, thyroid hormone brought the test into range without improving quality of life over 3 to 18 months [@feller-2018-subclinical].

| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| Aged 65 or more, TSH 4.60 to 19.99 mIU/l (737) | Symptom and tiredness scores at 1 year differed by 0.0 and 0.4 points; minimum important difference 9 [@stott-2017-trust] | Deaths 10 of 368 (2.7%) against 5 of 369 (1.4%); HR 1.91 (0.65 to 5.60) [@stott-2017-trust] |
| Aged 65 or more, two trials, median 17 months (842) | Cardiovascular events HR 0.74 (0.41 to 1.35) [@zijlstra-2021-cardiovascular] | Deaths HR 1.28 (0.54 to 3.03) [@zijlstra-2021-cardiovascular] |
| Euthyroid women with TPO antibodies and miscarriage or infertility (952) | Live births at 34 weeks or more, 37.4% against 37.9% [@dhillon-smith-2019-tablet] | Serious adverse events 5.9% against 3.8% [@dhillon-smith-2019-tablet] |

TRUST was underpowered for cardiovascular events and mortality, its authors state [@stott-2017-trust].

:::consensus
The 2019 BMJ panel recommended strongly against treating most adults with subclinical hypothyroidism; it found harms measured in only one trial [@bekkering-2019-subclinical].
:::

## Without the medicine

Selenium tablets did not improve well-being: in 412 Danish adults with autoimmune hypothyroidism on levothyroxine, 12-month quality-of-life scores were 28.8 against 28.0 on placebo [@larsen-2024-selenium].

:::unresolved
Long-term outcomes have been studied mostly in subclinical disease, because overt hypothyroidism is generally treated; myxoedema coma, from long-standing untreated severe hypothyroidism, carries 40% mortality despite treatment [@chaker-2017-hypothyroidism].
:::

## How the recommendations were set

TSH rises with age in people without thyroid disease [@xu-2026-thyroid-ageing].

Among 52,298 UK adults, median TSH at initiation fell from 8.7 to 7.9 mIU/l between 2001 and 2009 [@taylor-2014-treatment-threshold]. Of 58,706 US starters in 2008 to 2018, 30.5% had normal thyroid function [@brito-2021-levothyroxine-use].

:::consensus
A 2004 US panel did not recommend routine treatment at TSH 4.5 to 10 mIU/l [@surks-2004-subclinical-panel]. NICE (2019) advises considering treatment at 10 or more, judging the balance under 65 from committee experience [@nice-2019-thyroid-disease]. The 2013 ETA guideline cited a database study by two of its own authors [@pearce-2013-eta-subclinical].
:::

## Stopping

Some people stay in the normal range after stopping, more often after a mildly raised TSH than after overt hypothyroidism [@burgos-2021-discontinuation].

In 17 observational studies with 1103 patients, at moderate to high risk of bias, 35.6% first treated for subclinical and 11.8% for overt hypothyroidism stayed euthyroid [@burgos-2021-discontinuation]. In 370 Dutch adults aged 60 or more, open-label stepwise reduction left 95 (25.7%) off treatment at 1 year; 142 halted when TSH reached 10 mIU/l or more [@ravensberg-2026-discontinuation].

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.
