summary: Salbutamol can tighten the airways instead of opening them, cause lactic acidosis and low blood potassium and disturb heart rhythm; deaths have been reported with excessive use, and its use to delay labour has been linked to fluid on the lungs and heart attacks in mothers. Its receptor is also found in the heart, muscle, fat and brain, so doses can shift potassium, glucose and lactate across the body. It opens narrowed airways within five minutes but leaves airway inflammation untreated; in mild asthma from age 12, 109 in 1000 people using only a reliever of this kind needed steroid tablets or injections for an attack over up to a year, against 52 using a reliever that also contains a steroid.

## Serious harms

Salbutamol, albuterol in the US, is prescribed for narrowed airways in asthma and other reversible airway disease [@fda-2026-albuterol-label].

| Condition | How often | Who is at risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paradoxical bronchospasm; anaphylaxis | Not estimated | Often first use of a new canister | [@fda-2026-albuterol-label] |
| Lactic acidosis | 40 of 154 children (26%) at 4 hours; lactate ≥5.0 mmol/l in 97 of 324 (30%) in asthma trials | Nebulised delivery, adjusted OR 10 | [@ruman-colombier-2021-lactate; @liedtke-2019-hyperlactataemia] |
| Hypokalaemia | 168 of 433 children (38.8%); 5 below 2.5 mEq/l | Nebulised route, emergency or intensive care, age above 5 | [@hung-2020-hypokalaemia] |
| Atrial fibrillation, supraventricular tachycardia, QTc prolongation | Not estimated | Loop or thiazide diuretics; high doses | [@fda-2026-albuterol-nebuliser; @fda-2026-albuterol-label] |
| Ketoacidosis | Not estimated | Diabetes; large intravenous doses | [@fda-2026-albuterol-label] |
| Overdose, excessive use: seizures, arrhythmia, cardiac arrest, death | Not estimated; heart rate above 200/min in isolated cases aged 2 to 12 | Home nebuliser use; methylxanthines (animal data) | [@fda-2026-albuterol-label; @fda-2026-albuterol-nebuliser] |
| Death with 3 or more canisters a year (association) | 14.8% against 12.6% over 5 years; 59,533 aged 65 to 99 | 6 or more canisters: HR 1.56 | [@to-2022-older-asthma] |
| Maternal pulmonary oedema and infarction; fetal cardiomegaly | EU 2013: 98 cardiovascular reports, 2 fatal; 8 infant deaths | Use to delay labour, rising with duration | [@ema-2013-saba-obstetric; @mhra-2013-saba-tocolysis] |
| Stevens-Johnson syndrome, erythema multiforme | Rare | Children taking tablets | [@fda-2026-albuterol-tablets] |

Potassium possibly shifts into cells; the fall is usually transient, lowest a mean 12 hours after presentation [@fda-2026-albuterol-label; @hung-2020-hypokalaemia]. In 47 reported patients, lactate fell by 3.0 mmol/l or more, or to 2.5 mmol/l or less, after a median 13 hours (3 to 56) [@liedtke-2019-hyperlactataemia]. Excessive-use deaths are suspected to follow cardiac arrest in a severe attack with hypoxia [@fda-2026-albuterol-label]. None of 12 placebo-controlled trials of delaying labour reported cardiac arrest or intensive care admission [@neilson-2014-betamimetics].

:::unresolved
In pooled trials of 3 days to 1 year, cardiovascular events were more frequent (RR 2.54), mostly sinus tachycardia; only trials reporting an adverse event were included [@salpeter-2004-cardiovascular]. Major cardiovascular events were associated with starting the agonist in COPD (HR 2.02), not significantly in asthma, and with 3 or more canisters a year in Taiwan (2.82 against 0.99 per 100 person-years) [@amegadzie-2022-mace; @lai-2026-mace]. In Japanese reports, inhaled salbutamol was associated with Takotsubo cardiomyopathy [@sato-2022-takotsubo]. Pregnancy data are observational: a cleft palate association (OR 1.50; 28,481 cases) was unconfirmed in 519,242 births, and an autism association (1.29) was non-significant with confirmed maternal asthma [@fda-2026-albuterol-label; @howley-2020-birth-defects; @garne-2016-anomalies; @shakhshir-2026-neurodevelopment].
:::

## Other pathways it affects

The receptor salbutamol binds is found in many tissues besides airway muscle [@billington-2017-agonists; @fda-2026-albuterol-label].

- Lung: epithelium, glands, vessels and immune cells express it; an anti-inflammatory effect is not indicated [@billington-2017-agonists].
- Heart: beta2 receptors are 10% to 50% of cardiac beta-adrenoceptors; protein kinase A phosphorylates phospholamban, RyR2 and troponin I, and cyclic AMP activates the HCN4 pacemaker channel [@fda-2026-albuterol-label; @uniprot-2025-adrenoceptors].
- Muscle and potassium (animal): in rat muscle at high potassium, salbutamol with warming and lactic acid restored force, recovery depending on the Na+-K+ pump [@pedersen-2003-potassium].

::pathway{id="sodium-potassium-pump-cycle"}

- Muscle and heart (healthy people): oral 16 mg daily for 11 weeks in 26 trained men added 1.8 kg lean mass, blunted endurance gains and thickened heart walls on echocardiography, not magnetic resonance [@hostrup-2026-cardiac]; inhaled doses lowered fat mass in women only [@hostrup-2023-leanness].
- Glucose and lactate (people): glucose exceeded 11 mmol/l in 56 of 154 children, 95% of all 154 also given corticosteroid; lactate is ascribed to stimulated glycolysis, fatty acids inhibiting pyruvate dehydrogenase [@ruman-colombier-2021-lactate; @liedtke-2019-hyperlactataemia].

::pathway{id="glycolysis"}

- Brain (cells, animals): salbutamol lowered alpha-synuclein through reduced H3K27 acetylation at *SNCA*; clenbuterol protected dopaminergic neurons in MPTP-treated mice [@mittal-2017-parkinson].
- Ovary and fetus (animals): dietary doses caused benign mesovarian leiomyomas in rats, subcutaneous doses cleft palate in 4.5% and 9.3% of mouse fetuses [@fda-2026-albuterol-label].

:::industry
In ten men, intravenous salbutamol raised brown-fat glucose uptake; a GSK chair part-funded the study [@straat-2023-brown-fat].
:::

:::unresolved
Salbutamol use was associated with less Parkinson's disease in Norway (rate ratio 0.66), not significantly in a 2025 meta-analysis (0.88), where causation was judged unsupported [@mittal-2017-parkinson; @szmigiel-2025-parkinson].
:::

## What it is for and how it works

Inhaled salbutamol relaxes the muscle of narrowed airways within 5 minutes, for 3 to 6 hours [@billington-2017-agonists]. Its receptor couples to Gs; adenylyl cyclase forms cyclic AMP, and protein kinase A lowers myosin phosphorylation and cytosolic calcium [@billington-2017-agonists; @fda-2026-albuterol-label].

::pathway{id="beta2-adrenoceptor-camp"}

Receptor kinases and arrestin uncouple the occupied receptor from Gs, desensitising airway cells, tissue and mice [@billington-2017-agonists; @rosenbaum-2009-gpcr]; in people, bronchoprotection fell by 0.75 doubling doses with regular use (3 trials, 50 people) [@walters-2003-regular-saba].

:::unresolved
No tolerance was detected in studies of 165 and 108 patients, funding unread, against a 17.8% smaller response across 22 trials [@taylor-1998-regular-salbutamol; @csonka-2026-pef; @salpeter-2004-tolerance].
:::

## In the body

More salbutamol reaches the blood from a tablet than from inhaled doses [@fda-2026-albuterol-label; @fda-2026-albuterol-tablets].

Peak plasma levels were about 3 ng/ml after 12 inhaled puffs and 18 ng/ml after a 4 mg tablet; half-life is about 4.6 to 6 hours [@fda-2026-albuterol-label; @fda-2026-albuterol-tablets]. SULT1A3, most active in the small intestine, sulfates it [@ko-2012-sult1a3]. Nebulised doses above 3 mg raised children's heart rate by more than 10% without more bronchodilation [@fda-2026-albuterol-nebuliser]. Beta-blockers may provoke severe bronchospasm; loop or thiazide diuretics can worsen hypokalaemia and ECG change [@fda-2026-albuterol-label].

## Genetic variation

Routine genetic testing for this drug class is judged unwarranted by a pharmacology reference [@billington-2017-agonists].

:::industry
In a GSK part-funded meta-analysis, ADRB2 haplotypes were not associated with exacerbations in 973 young people using as-required short-acting agonists [@karimi-2021-haplotypes].
:::

## Benefit against harm

Salbutamol relieves symptoms but leaves airway inflammation untreated [@billington-2017-agonists; @ema-2023-salbutamol-overuse].

US approval rests on FEV1 over 2 to 12 weeks, with safety data from 248 recipients [@fda-2026-albuterol-label].

| Group | Benefit | Serious harm |
|---|---|---|
| Stable asthma: regular against as-needed use | Exacerbations unchanged (OR 0.86, 2922 people) [@walters-2003-regular-saba] | Tremor OR 13.47; few trials reported adverse events [@walters-2003-regular-saba] |
| Mild asthma, up to 52 weeks: reliever alone against as-needed budesonide-formoterol | Mortality and quality of life not different [@crossingham-2021-as-needed] | From age 12, exacerbations needing systemic corticosteroid 109 against 52 per 1000, one more per 18 [@crossingham-2021-as-needed]; aged 5 to 15, attacks 0.41 against 0.23 per child-year [@hatter-2025-care] |
| Children after a beclomethasone run-in excluding 555 of 843: salbutamol alone against daily beclomethasone, 44 weeks | Growth 1.1 cm greater [@martinez-2011-trexa] | First exacerbation 49% against 28%, one more per 5 [@martinez-2011-trexa] |

AstraZeneca wholly or partly funded both trials behind the figures from age 12 [@crossingham-2021-as-needed].

## Without the medicine

In a randomised trial of 655 adults, breathing retraining raised asthma quality-of-life scores by 0.28 and 0.24 at 12 months; attacks were not significantly fewer (9% and 11% against 15%) [@bruton-2018-breathe].

## How the recommendations were set

Reliever limits were set from studies linking heavy use with severe attacks and death [@ema-2023-salbutamol-overuse; @rcp-2014-nrad].

Among 195 UK asthma deaths, 65 of 165 (39%) were issued more than 12 relievers in the final year, without a comparison group [@rcp-2014-nrad]. In 2023 the EU warned that overuse may raise the risk of severe exacerbations and death, setting re-evaluation above twice-weekly use [@ema-2023-salbutamol-overuse].

:::consensus
GINA dropped the reliever alone for adults and adolescents in 2019, and BTS, NICE and SIGN at any age in 2024 [@reddel-2019-gina; @nice-2024-asthma-ng245].
:::

:::industry
In AstraZeneca's Swedish SABINA cohort, asthma was the recorded cause of 28 of 2564 deaths [@nwaru-2020-sabina].
:::

:::unresolved
Pooled mortality (RR 2.04, I² 98.3%) rests on three cohorts, two from SABINA; confounding by severity remains possible [@tsao-2025-saba-overuse; @zhao-2023-beta-agonist]. Both GINA chairs declared AstraZeneca fees in 2021, and three SYGMA 1 authors co-wrote GINA 2019 [@gina-2022-disclosures; @obyrne-2018-sygma1; @reddel-2019-gina].
:::

## Stopping

In two trials, airway calibre and responsiveness did not differ significantly 1 and 4 weeks after regular salbutamol was stopped [@walters-2003-regular-saba].

Needing more doses than usual calls for re-evaluation [@fda-2026-albuterol-label].

A prescription is changed together with the prescriber.
